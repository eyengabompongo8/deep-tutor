# CEFR A2 French Curriculum Specification

---

## Pedagogical Overview & Target Competencies
The CEFR A2 level (Waystage / Elementary) represents a crucial stage in French language acquisition. Learners transition from isolated formulaic phrases to constructing cohesive multi-clause sentences, expressing past events, immediate previous actions, upcoming future plans, daily routines, commands, and rich descriptive modifications.

This document provides the definitive curriculum specification and computational NLP parsing schema (using spaCy Matchers) for CEFR Level A2 French within the **FrenchTutor** application environment.

### Module A2.1: Reflexive Verbs & Imperative

#### Micro-topic A2.1.1: Present Tense Reflexive Verbs (*Verbes pronominaux au présent*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.1 Reflexive Verbs & Imperative  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/reflexive-verbs/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. Reflexive verbs (*verbes pronominaux*) express actions performed by the subject upon itself.
2. They require a reflexive pronoun matching the person and number of the subject:
   - `je` -> `me` (or `m'` before a vowel / silent h)
   - `tu` -> `te` (or `t'`)
   - `il / elle / on` -> `se` (or `s'`)
   - `nous` -> `nous`
   - `vous` -> `vous`
   - `ils / elles` -> `se` (or `s'`)
3. Word order in affirmative present declarative sentences: `Subject + Reflexive Pronoun + Conjugated Verb`.

##### Key Trigger Words & FLELex / PolylexFLE Vocabulary
- *se laver*, *se lever*, *se coucher*, *se réveiller*, *s'habiller*, *se préparer*, *se promener*, *s'appeler*.

##### Canonical Sentence Translation Table
| French Sentence | Spanish / English Translation | Targeted Structure & Analysis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Je me lave les mains.* | ES: Me lavo las manos. / EN: I wash my hands. | Subject `Je` + reflexive pronoun `me` + conjugated verb `lave`. |
| *Tu te lèves tôt le matin.* | ES: Te levantas temprano por la mañana. / EN: You get up early in the morning. | 2nd person singular pronoun `te` + present tense `lèves`. |
| *Elle s'habille rapidement.* | ES: Ella se viste rápidamente. / EN: She gets dressed quickly. | Elided reflexive pronoun `s'` before vowel-initial verb `habille`. |
| *Nous nous promenons dans le parc.* | ES: Nos paseamos por el parque. / EN: We take a walk in the park. | Reduplicated 1st person plural pronoun `nous nous`. |
| *Vous vous préparez pour le départ.* | ES: Uds. se preparan para la salida. / EN: You prepare yourselves for departure. | 2nd person plural pronoun `vous vous` + present verb `préparez`. |

#### Micro-topic A2.1.2: Daily Routine Reflexive Expressions (*Verbes pronominaux de la routine quotidienne*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.1 Reflexive Verbs & Imperative  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/reflexive-verbs/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. Body parts in reflexive daily routine actions use **definite articles** (`le`, `la`, `les`, `l'`) rather than possessive adjectives.
2. In French, ownership of the body part is already established by the reflexive pronoun (e.g., *Je me lave les cheveux*, NOT *mes cheveux*).

##### Key Trigger Words & FLELex / PolylexFLE Vocabulary
- *se brosser les dents*, *se peigner les cheveux*, *se laver le visage*, *se raser*, *se maquiller*, *s'endormir*.

##### Canonical Sentence Translation Table
| French Sentence | Spanish / English Translation | Targeted Structure & Analysis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Il se brosse les dents après chaque repas.* | ES: Él se cepilla los dientes después de cada comida. / EN: He brushes his teeth after every meal. | Reflexive verb `se brosser` + definite article `les` + body part noun `dents`. |
| *Je me lave le visage à l'eau froide.* | ES: Me lavo la cara con agua fría. / EN: I wash my face with cold water. | `Je me lave` + definite article `le` + body part noun `visage`. |
| *Elles se peignent les cheveux.* | ES: Ellas se peinan el cabello. / EN: They comb their hair. | 3rd person plural `se peignent` + definite article `les`. |
| *Mon père se rase tous les matins.* | ES: Mi padre se afeita todas las mañanas. / EN: My father shaves every morning. | Daily routine temporal frequency + reflexive verb `se rase`. |
| *Elle s'endort vers minuit.* | ES: Ella se duerme cerca de la medianoche. / EN: She falls asleep around midnight. | Irregular reflexive verb `s'endormir` in present tense. |

#### Micro-topic A2.1.3: Negation of Reflexive Verbs (*Négation des verbes pronominaux*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.1 Reflexive Verbs & Imperative  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/reflexive-verbs/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. Negation wraps around BOTH the reflexive pronoun and the conjugated verb.
2. Structure: `Subject + ne / n' + Reflexive Pronoun + Conjugated Verb + pas / plus / jamais`.

##### Key Trigger Words & FLELex / PolylexFLE Vocabulary
- *ne...pas*, *ne...jamais*, *ne...plus*, *ne...rien*.

##### Canonical Sentence Translation Table
| French Sentence | Spanish / English Translation | Targeted Structure & Analysis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Je ne me réveille pas tard.* | ES: No me despierto tarde. / EN: I do not wake up late. | `ne` + reflexive pronoun `me` + verb `réveille` + `pas`. |
| *Tu ne t'habilles pas en noir.* | ES: No te vistes de negro. / EN: You do not dress in black. | `ne` + elided `t'` + verb + `pas`. |
| *Il ne se couche jamais avant 23h.* | ES: Él nunca se acuesta antes de las 23h. / EN: He never goes to bed before 11pm. | Adverb `jamais` replacing `pas` around reflexive verb block. |
| *Nous ne nous disputons plus.* | ES: Ya no discutimos. / EN: We do not argue anymore. | `ne` + `nous` + verb `disputons` + `plus`. |
| *Vous ne vous souvenez pas de lui.* | ES: Uds. no se acuerdan de él. / EN: You do not remember him. | Negation of idiomatic reflexive verb *se souvenir*. |

#### Micro-topic A2.1.4: Affirmative Imperative Mood (*L'impératif affirmatif*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.1 Reflexive Verbs & Imperative  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/imperative/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. The imperative mood is used to express commands, requests, or advice.
2. It exists only for three grammatical persons: `tu` (2nd sing.), `nous` (1st pl.), and `vous` (2nd pl.).
3. **No subject pronouns** are expressed in imperative sentences.
4. For all -ER verbs (including *aller*), the final *-s* of the `tu` form is dropped (e.g., *Parle!*, *Mange!*, *Va!*), unless followed by adverbial pronouns *y* or *en*.

##### Key Trigger Words & FLELex / PolylexFLE Vocabulary
- *Écoute!*, *Regardez!*, *Mangeons!*, *Fais!*, *Prenez!*, *Allons-y!*.

##### Canonical Sentence Translation Table
| French Sentence | Spanish / English Translation | Targeted Structure & Analysis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Écoute la musique attentivement!* | ES: ¡Escucha la música atentamente! / EN: Listen to the music attentively! | 2nd person singular imperative of -ER verb (`tu` form dropping final *-s*). |
| *Mangeons le dîner ensemble!* | ES: ¡Comamos la cena juntos! / EN: Let's eat dinner together! | 1st person plural imperative (`nous` form). |
| *Regardez ce magnifique tableau!* | ES: ¡Miren este magnífico cuadro! / EN: Look at this magnificent painting! | 2nd person plural/formal imperative (`vous` form). |
| *Fais tes devoirs avant de sortir!* | ES: ¡Haz tus deberes antes de salir! / EN: Do your homework before going out! | Irregular imperative of *faire* (`tu` form retaining *-s*). |
| *Prenez votre manteau!* | ES: ¡Tome su abrigo! / EN: Take your coat! | 2nd person plural imperative of *prendre*. |

#### Micro-topic A2.1.5: Negative Imperative Mood (*L'impératif négatif*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.1 Reflexive Verbs & Imperative  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/imperative/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. The negative imperative wraps *ne / n'* before the imperative verb form and *pas / plus / jamais* after it.
2. Structure: `ne / n' + Imperative Verb + pas / plus / jamais`.
3. Object pronouns remain BEFORE the verb in negative imperative commands (e.g., *Ne le touche pas!*).

##### Key Trigger Words & FLELex / PolylexFLE Vocabulary
- *Ne parle pas!*, *Ne faites pas!*, *Ne cours pas!*, *N'oublie pas!*, *Ne soyez pas!*.

##### Canonical Sentence Translation Table

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Ne parle pas pendant le cours!* | ES: ¡No hables durante la clase! / EN: Do not speak during class! | `ne` + imperative verb `parle` + `pas`. |
| *Ne faisons pas trop de bruit!* | ES: ¡No hagamos demasiado ruido! / EN: Let's not make too much noise! | `ne` + 1st person plural imperative `faisons` + `pas`. |
| *Ne partez pas sans moi!* | ES: ¡No se vayan sin mí! / EN: Do not leave without me! | `ne` + 2nd person plural imperative `partez` + `pas`. |
| *N'oublie pas tes clés!* | ES: ¡No olvides tus llaves! / EN: Don't forget your keys! | Elided `n'` before vowel + imperative verb `oublie`. |
| *Ne soyez pas en retard!* | ES: ¡No lleguen tarde! / EN: Don't be late! | Irregular imperative of *être* (*soyez*). |

#### Micro-topic A2.1.6: Imperative of Reflexive Verbs (*L'impératif des verbes pronominaux*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.1 Reflexive Verbs & Imperative  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/imperative/`, `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/reflexive-verbs/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. **Affirmative Reflexive Imperative:**
   - The reflexive pronoun is attached AFTER the verb with a hyphen.
   - The 2nd person singular pronoun `te` becomes the stressed tonic pronoun `toi` (e.g., *Lève-toi!*).
   - Forms: `Verb-toi!`, `Verb-nous!`, `Verb-vous!`.
2. **Negative Reflexive Imperative:**
   - Standard pre-verbal pronoun position is maintained: `ne + pronoun (te, nous, vous) + verb + pas`.
   - `te` remains `te` (or `t'`) before the verb (e.g., *Ne te lève pas!*).

##### Key Trigger Words & FLELex / PolylexFLE Vocabulary
- *Lève-toi!*, *Couchons-nous!*, *Asseyez-vous!*, *Ne te presse pas!*, *Ne vous inquiétez pas!*.

##### Canonical Sentence Translation Table
| French Sentence | Spanish / English Translation | Targeted Structure & Analysis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Lève-toi vite, il est tard!* | ES: ¡Levántate rápido, es tarde! / EN: Get up quickly, it is late! | Affirmative imperative + hyphen + tonic reflexive pronoun `toi`. |
| *Ne te lève pas si tôt!* | ES: ¡No te levantes tan temprano! / EN: Don't get up so early! | Negative imperative with pre-verbal pronoun `te`. |
| *Habillons-nous pour la fête!* | ES: ¡Vistámonos para la fiesta! / EN: Let's dress up for the party! | 1st person plural affirmative imperative + hyphen + `nous`. |
| *Asseyez-vous, s'il vous plaît!* | ES: ¡Siéntense, por favor! / EN: Please sit down! | 2nd person plural affirmative imperative + hyphen + `vous`. |
| *Ne vous inquiétez pas!* | ES: ¡No se preocupen! / EN: Don't worry! | Negative imperative with pre-verbal `vous`. |

### Module A2.2: Passé Composé & Recent Past

#### Micro-topic A2.2.1: Passé Composé with *Avoir* (*Le passé composé avec l'auxiliaire avoir*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.2 Passé Composé & Recent Past  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/passe-compose/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. The passé composé expresses completed actions in the past.
2. Most French verbs use the present tense of *avoir* as auxiliary: `j'ai`, `tu as`, `il/elle a`, `nous avons`, `vous avez`, `ils/elles ont`.
3. Regular past participle endings:
   - -ER verbs -> *-é* (*parler* -> *parlé*)
   - -IR verbs -> *-i* (*finir* -> *fini*)
   - -RE verbs -> *-u* (*vendre* -> *vendu*)
4. **No agreement** of the past participle with the subject when *avoir* is used.

##### Key Trigger Words & FLELex / PolylexFLE Vocabulary
- *avoir*, *hier*, *la semaine dernière*, *l'année passée*, *déjà*, *enfin*.

##### Canonical Sentence Translation Table
| French Sentence | Spanish / English Translation | Targeted Structure & Analysis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *J'ai parlé avec le professeur hier.* | ES: Hablé con el profesor ayer. / EN: I spoke with the teacher yesterday. | Auxiliary `ai` (avoir) + regular -ER past participle `parlé`. |
| *Tu as fini ton travail à temps.* | ES: Terminaste tu trabajo a tiempo. / EN: You finished your work on time. | Auxiliary `as` + regular -IR past participle `fini`. |
| *Il a vendu sa vieille voiture.* | ES: Él vendió su viejo coche. / EN: He sold his old car. | Auxiliary `a` + regular -RE past participle `vendu`. |
| *Nous avons mangé une pizza.* | ES: Comimos una pizza. / EN: We ate a pizza. | 1st person plural auxiliary `avons` + past participle `mangé`. |
| *Ils ont cherché la solution partout.* | ES: Buscaron la solución en todas partes. / EN: They looked for the solution everywhere. | 3rd person plural auxiliary `ont` + past participle `cherché`. |

#### Micro-topic A2.2.2: Passé Composé with *Être* & Motion Verbs (*Le passé composé avec être*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.2 Passé Composé & Recent Past  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/etre-verbs-passe-compose/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. A specific list of intransitive motion and change-of-state verbs (DR & MRS VANDERTRAMPP) use *être* as auxiliary: `je suis`, `tu es`, `il/elle est`, `nous sommes`, `vous êtes`, `ils/elles sont`.
2. Core verbs: *aller*, *venir*, *partir*, *arriver*, *entrer*, *sortir*, *monter*, *descendre*, *tomber*, *naître*, *mourir*, *rester*, *retourner*, *devenir*.
3. **Mandatory Subject Agreement:** The past participle MUST agree in gender (+e) and number (+s) with the subject.

##### Key Trigger Words & FLELex / PolylexFLE Vocabulary
- *aller*, *venir*, *partir*, *arriver*, *entrer*, *sortir*, *monter*, *descendre*, *tomber*, *naître*, *mourir*, *rester*, *retourner*.

##### Canonical Sentence Translation Table
| French Sentence | Spanish / English Translation | Targeted Structure & Analysis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Elle est allée en France l'été dernier.* | ES: Ella fue a Francia el verano pasado. / EN: She went to France last summer. | Auxiliary `est` (être) + past participle `allée` (agrees with feminine *Elle*). |
| *Ils sont venus à la réunion.* | ES: Vinieron a la reunión. / EN: They came to the meeting. | Auxiliary `sont` + past participle `venus` (masculine plural agreement). |
| *Nous sommes partis très tôt.* | ES: Salimos muy temprano. / EN: We left very early. | 1st person plural auxiliary `sommes` + agreed participle `partis`. |
| *Mon frère est né en 1998.* | ES: Mi hermano nació en 1998. / EN: My brother was born in 1998. | Auxiliary `est` + past participle `né` of life change verb. |
| *Elle est tombée dans l'escalier.* | ES: Ella se cayó en la escalera. / EN: She fell down the stairs. | Auxiliary `est` + past participle `tombée` (feminine singular agreement). |

#### Micro-topic A2.2.3: Irregular Past Participles (*Participes passés irréguliers*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.2 Passé Composé & Recent Past  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/past-participles/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. High-frequency irregular verbs have unique past participle forms that do not follow standard -é, -i, -u paradigms.
2. Key irregular stems:
   - *avoir* -> *eu*
   - *être* -> *été*
   - *faire* -> *fait*
   - *voir* -> *vu*
   - *prendre* -> *pris*
   - *mettre* -> *mis*
   - *pouvoir* -> *pu*
   - *vouloir* -> *voulu*
   - *devoir* -> *dû*
   - *lire* -> *lu*
   - *écrire* -> *écrit*
   - *dire* -> *dit*

##### Key Trigger Words & FLELex / PolylexFLE Vocabulary
- *eu*, *été*, *fait*, *vu*, *pris*, *mis*, *pu*, *voulu*, *dû*, *lu*, *écrit*, *dit*.

##### Canonical Sentence Translation Table
| French Sentence | Spanish / English Translation | Targeted Structure & Analysis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *J'ai fait tous mes devoirs.* | ES: Hice todos mis deberes. / EN: I did all my homework. | Auxiliary `ai` + irregular past participle `fait` (faire). |
| *Elle a lu un roman passionnant.* | ES: Ella leyó una novela apasionante. / EN: She read an exciting novel. | Auxiliary `a` + irregular past participle `lu` (lire). |
| *Nous avons pris le train de 8h.* | ES: Tomamos el tren de las 8. / EN: We took the 8 o'clock train. | Auxiliary `avons` + irregular past participle `pris` (prendre). |
| *Ils ont écrit une lettre recommandée.* | ES: Escribieron una carta certificada. / EN: They wrote a registered letter. | Auxiliary `ont` + irregular past participle `écrit` (écrire). |
| *As-tu vu ce nouveau film?* | ES: ¿Has visto esta nueva película? / EN: Have you seen this new movie? | Inverted auxiliary `As-tu` + irregular past participle `vu` (voir). |

#### Micro-topic A2.2.4: Past Participle Agreement Rules (*Accord du participe passé*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.2 Passé Composé & Recent Past  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/past-participles/`, `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/etre-verbs-passe-compose/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. **Auxiliary Être:** Past participle ALWAYS agrees in gender (*+e*) and number (*+s*) with the grammatical subject.
2. **Auxiliary Avoir:** Past participle NEVER agrees with the subject.
3. **Preceding Direct Object Rule (Avoir):** The past participle agrees in gender and number with the Direct Object (*Complément d'Objet Direct - COD*) ONLY IF the COD precedes the verb (via direct pronouns *le*, *la*, *les*, *que*, or interrogative nouns).

##### Key Trigger Words & FLELex / PolylexFLE Vocabulary

#### Micro-topic A2.3.4: Formation and Placement of Manner Adverbs in *-ment* (*Adverbes en -ment*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.3 Near Future & Modifiers/Adjectives  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/adverbs/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. Manner adverbs ending in *-ment* are formed from adjectives:
   - Add *-ment* to feminine singular adjective stem (*lent* -> *lente* -> *lentement*; *heureux* -> *heureuse* -> *heureusement*).

2. **Placement Rules:**
   - In simple tenses, adverbs immediately follow the conjugated verb.
   - In compound tenses (passé composé), short/common adverbs sit **between auxiliary and past participle** (e.g., *Il a rapidement répondu*).

### Module A2.4: Object Pronouns & Time Expressions

#### Micro-topic A2.4.1: Imperfect Tense Introduction (*L'imparfait - Formation et emploi*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.4 Object Pronouns & Time Expressions  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/imperfect-tense/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. **Formation of l'imparfait:**
   - **Stem:** Take the present indicative *nous* form of the verb and drop the *-ons* ending.
     - *parler* -> *nous parlons* -> stem `parl-`
     - *finir* -> *nous finissons* -> stem `finiss-`
     - *faire* -> *nous faisons* -> stem `fais-`
     - *prendre* -> *nous prenons* -> stem `pren-`
   - **Endings:** Append the universal set of imperfect endings for all verb groups:
     - `je` -> `-ais`
     - `tu` -> `-ais`
     - `il / elle / on` -> `-ait`
     - `nous` -> `-ions`
     - `vous` -> `-iez`
     - `ils / elles` -> `-aient`
   - **Irregular Stem:** The verb *être* is the sole verb with an irregular stem (`ét-`), to which standard endings attach: *j'étais*, *tu étais*, *il/elle/on était*, *nous étions*, *vous étiez*, *ils/elles étaient*.
   - **Spelling Orthographic Adjustments:**
     - Verbs ending in *-ger* (e.g., *manger*, *voyager*) retain the `-e-` before endings starting with `-a-` (*je mangeais*, *tu mangeais*, *il mangeait*, *ils mangeaient*), but drop it before `-i-` (*nous mangions*, *vous mangiez*).
     - Verbs ending in *-cer* (e.g., *lancer*, *commencer*) change `c` to `ç` before endings starting with `-a-` (*je me lançais*, *il commençait*), but maintain `c` before `-i-` (*nous me lancions*).
     - Verbs in *-ier* (e.g., *étudier*) have double `-ii-` in *nous* and *vous* forms (*nous étudiions*, *vous étudiez* -> *vous étudiiez*).

2. **Core Aspectual Uses:**
   - **Habitual or Repeated Past Actions:** Expresses actions performed habitually or repeatedly in the past ("used to do", "would do").
   - **Background Description & Context:** Sets the background, atmosphere, physical state, age, weather, or emotional state in past narrative descriptions.
   - **Ongoing Unfinished Past States / Actions in Progress:** Represents actions in progress at a given time in the past ("was/were doing"), frequently serving as the background for an interrupting event in the *passé composé*.

3. **Key Trigger Adverbs & Time Expressions:**
   - Adverbs signaling past habits or ongoing states: *toujours* (always), *souvent* (often), *d'habitude* (usually / habitually), *autrefois* (in the past / formerly), *tous les jours* (every day), *chaque soir* (every evening), *pendant que* (while).

##### Key Trigger Words & FLELex / PolylexFLE Vocabulary
- *toujours*, *souvent*, *d'habitude*, *autrefois*, *tous les jours*, *chaque soir*, *pendant que*, *était*, *faisait*, *avait*.

#### Micro-topic A2.4.2: Simple Future Tense Introduction (*Le futur simple*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.4 Object Pronouns & Time Expressions  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/future-tense/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. **Formation of Regular Verbs:**
   - For regular -ER and -IR verbs, the stem is the full infinitive form (e.g., *parler-* -> *parlerai*, *finir-* -> *finirai*).
   - For regular -RE verbs, the final *-e* is dropped from the infinitive to form the stem (e.g., *vendre-* -> *vendrai*).
2. **Futur Simple Endings:** Standard future tense endings are appended to the stem for all verb groups:
   - `je` -> `-ai`
   - `tu` -> `-as`
   - `il / elle / on` -> `-a`
   - `nous` -> `-ons`
   - `vous` -> `-ez`
   - `ils / elles` -> `-ont`
   *(Note: These endings mirror the present tense conjugation of the auxiliary verb *avoir*).*
3. **Irregular Stems:** Core irregular verbs maintain special future stems (all ending in *-r-*) to which standard endings are added:
   - *être* -> `ser-` (*je serai*)
   - *avoir* -> `aur-` (*j'aurai*)
   - *aller* -> `ir-` (*j'irai*)
   - *faire* -> `fer-` (*je ferai*)
   - *venir* -> `viend-` (*je viendrai*)
   - *devoir* -> `devr-` (*je devrai*)
   - *pouvoir* -> `pourr-` (*je pourrai*)
   - *vouloir* -> `voudr-` (*je voudrai*)
4. **Contrast with Futur Proche:**
   - **Futur Proche** (`aller` in present + infinitive) indicates immediate intentions, imminent actions, or informal spoken plans (*Je vais parler*).
   - **Futur Simple** (infinitive/irregular stem + endings) expresses formal/written context, long-term projections, predictions, or conditional future events (*Je parlerai*).

##### Key Trigger Words & FLELex / PolylexFLE Vocabulary
- *demain*, *plus tard*, *l'année prochaine*, *dans le futur*, *bientôt*, *serai*, *aurai*, *irai*, *ferai*, *viendrai*, *devrai*, *pourrai*, *voudrai*.

#### Micro-topic A2.4.3: Time and Duration Expressions (*Les expressions de temps et de durée: depuis, pendant, il y a, dans*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.4 Object Pronouns & Time Expressions  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/prepositions/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. **Depuis (Ongoing Action / Duration & Starting Point):**
   - Expresses an action that started in the past and continues into the present moment.
   - Requires the **Present Indicative** tense (*présent de l'indicatif*).
   - Used with a time duration (*depuis deux mois* = for two months) or a specific starting point (*depuis 2020* = since 2020).
   - Structure: `Present Verb + depuis + Time Duration / Starting Point`.

2. **Pendant (Completed Duration):**
   - Expresses a bounded, completed duration in the past or future with no ongoing connection to the present.
   - Typically used with the **Passé Composé** for completed past events, or **Futur Simple** for bounded future events.
   - Structure: `Passé Composé / Future Verb + pendant + Time Duration`.

3. **Il y a (Elapsed Time Ago):**
   - Expresses elapsed time prior to the present moment ("ago").
   - Requires a **Past Tense** (commonly *Passé Composé*).
   - Structure: `Passé Composé Verb + il y a + Time Duration`.

4. **Dans (Future Delay):**
   - Expresses the amount of time before a future action will begin ("in [time]").
   - Requires the **Futur Simple** or **Futur Proche** (or present with future meaning).
   - Structure: `Future Verb + dans + Time Duration`.

##### Key Trigger Words & FLELex / PolylexFLE Vocabulary
- *depuis*, *pendant*, *il y a*, *dans*, *minute*, *heure*, *jour*, *semaine*, *mois*, *an*, *année*.

| *Elle a habité à Paris pendant deux ans.* | ES: Vivió en París durante dos años. / EN: She lived in Paris for two years. | Passé composé `a habité` + `pendant` + completed duration `deux ans`. |
| *Nous sommes arrivés il y a trois heures.* | ES: Llegamos hace tres horas. / EN: We arrived three hours ago. | Passé composé `sommes arrivés` + temporal expression `il y a` + duration `trois heures`. |

#### Micro-topic A2.4.4: Extended Negation (*La négation étendue: ne...jamais, ne...rien, ne...personne, ne...plus, ne...que*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.4 Object Pronouns & Time Expressions  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/negation/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. **Core Negative Structure:**
   - Extended negative words replace *pas* in the negative sandwich around the verb.
   - Standard structure in simple tenses (Present, Imparfait, Futur simple):
     `Subject + ne / n' + [Object/Reflexive Pronoun] + Conjugated Verb + jamais / rien / personne / plus / que`.

2. **Word Order in Simple vs. Compound Tenses (e.g., Passé Composé):**
   - **Mid-Auxiliary Placement (`jamais`, `rien`, `plus`):**
     In compound tenses, `jamais`, `rien`, and `plus` are placed **between the auxiliary verb and the past participle**:
     `Subject + ne / n' + Auxiliary (avoir / être) + jamais / rien / plus + Past Participle`.
     - *Ex:* *Je n'ai rien compris.* (I didn't understand anything.)
     - *Ex:* *Elle n'a jamais mangé de foie gras.* (She has never eaten foie gras.)
     - *Ex:* *Il n'a plus fumé.* (He hasn't smoked anymore.)
   - **Post-Participle Placement (`personne`):**
     Unlike `jamais/rien/plus`, `personne` is placed **after the past participle**:
     `Subject + ne / n' + Auxiliary + Past Participle + personne`.
     - *Ex:* *Je n'ai vu personne.* (I didn't see anyone.) — *Incorrect:* \*Je n'ai personne vu.
   - **Restrictive Placement (`ne...que`):**
     The restrictive word `que` (meaning "only") is placed **immediately before the restricted element** (noun, prepositional phrase, time expression) post-verbally:
     `Subject + ne / n' + Verb / Auxiliary + [Past Participle] + que + Restricted Element`.
     - *Ex:* *Il ne boit que de l'eau.* (He only drinks water.)
     - *Ex:* *Elle n'a acheté que deux livres.* (She only bought two books.)

3. **Subject Position for `personne` and `rien`:**
   - Both `personne` (nobody) and `rien` (nothing) can function as the **grammatical subject** of the sentence.
   - In subject position, `pas` is omitted and `ne / n'` immediately follows the subject before the conjugated verb:
     - `Personne + ne / n' + Conjugated Verb...`
       - *Ex:* *Personne ne vient ce soir.* (Nobody is coming tonight.)
     - `Rien + ne / n' + Conjugated Verb...`
       - *Ex:* *Rien ne marche ici.* (Nothing works here.)

4. **Restriction `ne...que` (Limitation / Exclusivity):**
   - `ne...que` is not a true negation; it does not deny the action but restricts it ("only" / "nothing but").
   - Consequently, indefinite and partitive articles (`un`, `une`, `des`, `du`, `de la`) are retained after `que`, unlike standard negations which convert indefinite/partitive articles to `de / d'`.

#### Micro-topic A2.4.6: Interrogative Pronouns (*Les pronoms interrogatifs: lequel, laquelle, lesquels, lesquelles*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.4 Object Pronouns & Time Expressions  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/interrogative-pronouns/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. **Definition & Function:** French interrogative pronouns (*lequel*, *laquelle*, *lesquels*, *lesquelles*) translate to "which one?" or "which ones?". They replace a specific noun phrase rather than modifying a noun directly, enabling speakers to select or inquire about specific items from a known group.
2. **Gender and Number Agreement:** Interrogative pronouns must agree strictly in gender and number with the noun they replace:
   - **Masculine Singular:** *lequel* (which one)
   - **Feminine Singular:** *laquelle* (which one)
   - **Masculine Plural:** *lesquels* (which ones)
   - **Feminine Plural:** *lesquelles* (which ones)
3. **Contraction with Prepositions (*à* and *de*):**
   - When preceded by the preposition **à** (to / at / in):
     - *à + lequel* -> **auquel**
     - *à + laquelle* -> **à laquelle** (no contraction)
     - *à + lesquels* -> **auxquels**
     - *à + lesquelles* -> **auxquelles**
   - When preceded by the preposition **de** (of / about / from):
     - *de + lequel* -> **duquel**
     - *de + laquelle* -> **de laquelle** (no contraction)
     - *de + lesquels* -> **desquels**
     - *de + lesquelles* -> **desquelles**
4. **Partitive / Selection Construction (*de* + noun/demonstrative):** Interrogative pronouns are frequently followed by `de` + determiner + noun (e.g., *Lequel de ces films...*) or `de` + demonstrative pronoun (e.g., *Laquelle de celles-ci...*) to denote selection from a group.

#### Micro-topic A2.4.5: Direct Object Pronouns (*Les pronoms compléments d'objet direct: le, la, l', les*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.4 Object Pronouns & Time Expressions  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/direct-object-pronouns/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. **Definition & Function:** Direct Object Pronouns (*pronoms compléments d'objet direct - COD*) replace a person or thing that receives the action of the verb directly, without an intervening preposition (*à*, *de*, etc.).
   - `le` (masculine singular) -> replaces a masculine singular noun (e.g., *le livre* -> *le*).
   - `la` (feminine singular) -> replaces a feminine singular noun (e.g., *la lettre* -> *la*).
   - `l'` (elided form) -> replaces `le` or `la` before a verb or auxiliary beginning with a vowel or silent *h*.
   - `les` (plural) -> replaces masculine or feminine plural nouns (e.g., *les clés* -> *les*).
   - First & Second person COD forms: `me` / `m'`, `te` / `t'`, `nous`, `vous`.
2. **Placement in Simple Tenses (Present, Imparfait, Futur Simple):**
   - The direct object pronoun is placed immediately **before the conjugated verb**.
   - Formula: `Subject + (ne) + COD Pronoun + Conjugated Verb + (pas)`.
   - Examples: *Je le vois tous les jours*, *Elle ne la regarde pas*.
3. **Placement in Compound Tenses (Passé Composé):**
   - The direct object pronoun is placed immediately **before the auxiliary verb** (*avoir* or *être*), NOT before the past participle.
   - Formula: `Subject + (ne) + COD Pronoun + Auxiliary + (pas) + Past Participle`.
   - **Mandatory Past Participle Agreement:** In compound tenses with *avoir*, the past participle MUST agree in gender (+e) and number (+s) with the preceding direct object pronoun.
   - Examples: *Je l'ai vue hier* (feminine singular agreement), *Nous ne les avons pas achetés* (masculine plural agreement).
4. **Placement with Infinitives (Futur Proche & Modal Verb Constructions):**
   - When a conjugated verb is followed by an infinitive (e.g., *aller*, *pouvoir*, *vouloir*, *devoir*, *savoir* + infinitive), the direct object pronoun is placed immediately **before the infinitive** verb governing the action.
   - Formula: `Subject + (ne + Conjugated Verb + pas) + COD Pronoun + Infinitive`.
   - Examples: *Je vais le lire ce soir*, *Il ne veut pas la perdre*.
5. **Negation Placement Summary:**
   - Simple Tenses: `ne` precedes the pronoun, `pas` follows the conjugated verb (`ne + COD + Verb + pas`).
   - Compound Tenses: `ne` precedes the pronoun, `pas` follows the auxiliary (`ne + COD + Auxiliary + pas + Participle`).
   - Infinitive Constructions: `ne...pas` wraps around the main conjugated/modal verb, leaving the pronoun directly before the infinitive (`ne + Main Verb + pas + COD + Infinitive`).

#### Micro-topic A2.4.7: Indirect Object Pronouns (*Les pronoms objets indirects : lui, leur, me, te, nous, vous*)
**CEFR Level:** A2  
**Module:** A2.4 Object Pronouns & Time Expressions  
**Source:** `https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/indirect-object-pronouns/`

##### Morphosyntactic Rules
1. **Definition & Function of Indirect Object Pronouns (COI - *Complément d'Objet Indirect*):**
   - An indirect object pronoun replaces a noun referring to a person or animate entity introduced by the preposition **à** after a verb.
   - Complete inventory of COI forms:
     - `me` / `m'` (1st sing. — to me)
     - `te` / `t'` (2nd sing. — to you)
     - `lui` (3rd sing. masc./fem. — to him / to her)
     - `nous` (1st pl. — to us)
     - `vous` (2nd pl. — to you)
     - `leur` (3rd pl. masc./fem. — to them)

2. **Distinction between COI and Direct Object Pronouns (COD):**
   - **COD (Direct Object):** Replaces nouns connected directly to the verb *without* a preposition (e.g., *aimer*, *voir*, *regarder*, *écouter*, *attendre*, *inviter*, *connaître*). 3rd person pronouns: `le`, `la`, `les`.
     - *Ex:* *Je vois Marc* -> *Je le vois.*
   - **COI (Indirect Object):** Replaces nouns introduced by preposition **à** (e.g., *parler à*, *téléphoner à*, *répondre à*). 3rd person pronouns: `lui` (singular, both genders) and `leur` (plural, both genders).
     - *Ex:* *Je parle à Marc* -> *Je lui parle.*
     - *Ex:* *Je parle à Marie* -> *Je lui parle.* (`lui` = to him / to her)
     - *Ex:* *Je parle à mes amis* -> *Je leur parle.* (`leur` = to them)
   - Note: 1st and 2nd person forms (`me`, `te`, `nous`, `vous`) are identical for both COD and COI.

3. **Verbs Requiring Preposition *à* + Animate Object (COI Verbs):**
   - Key A2 French verbs that take an indirect object introduced by *à*:
     - *parler à quelqu'un* (to speak/talk to someone)
     - *téléphoner à quelqu'un* (to phone/call someone)
     - *répondre à quelqu'un* (to answer someone)
     - *écrire à quelqu'un* (to write to someone)
     - *donner quelque chose à quelqu'un* (to give something to someone)
     - *envoyer quelque chose à quelqu'un* (to send something to someone)
     - *expliquer quelque chose à quelqu'un* (to explain something to someone)
     - *demander quelque chose à quelqu'un* (to ask someone for something)
     - *montrer quelque chose à quelqu'un* (to show something to someone)
     - *sourire à quelqu'un* (to smile at someone)
     - *offrir quelque chose à quelqu'un* (to offer/give something to someone)
     - *souhaiter quelque chose à quelqu'un* (to wish something to someone)
     - *dire quelque chose à quelqu'un* (to say/tell something to someone)

4. **Word Order & Placement Rules across Syntactic Contexts:**
   - **Simple Tenses (Present, Imparfait, Futur Simple):** `Subject + COI Pronoun + Conjugated Verb`
     - *Ex:* *Je lui parle.* / *Elle nous répond.*
   - **Negation in Simple Tenses:** `Subject + ne / n' + COI Pronoun + Conjugated Verb + pas / plus / jamais`
     - *Ex:* *Je ne lui parle pas.* / *Elle ne leur répond jamais.*
   - **Compound Tenses (Passé Composé):** `Subject + COI Pronoun + Auxiliary (avoir/être) + Past Participle`
     - *Ex:* *Je lui ai téléphoné.* / *Il leur a écrit.*
     - **CRITICAL AGREEMENT RULE:** Unlike COD pronouns, COI pronouns do **NOT** trigger past participle agreement! (*Elle lui a parlé* — no `-e` added to *parlé*).
   - **Dual Verb / Infinitive Construction (Futur Proche, Modals + Infinitive):** `Subject + Conjugated Verb + COI Pronoun + Infinitive`
     - *Ex:* *Je vais lui parler.* / *Tu peux leur répondre.*
     - Negation: *Je ne vais pas lui parler.*
   - **Imperative Mood:**
     - **Affirmative Imperative:** `Verb-COI Pronoun` (post-verbal with hyphen). `me` becomes `moi`, `te` becomes `toi`.

     - **Negative Imperative:** `ne / n' + COI Pronoun + Verb + pas` (standard pre-verbal placement).
       - *Ex:* *Ne me parle pas !* / *Ne lui téléphone pas !*
