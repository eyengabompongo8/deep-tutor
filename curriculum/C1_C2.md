## Module C1_C2.1: Advanced & Literary Subjunctive Moods

### Topic ID: C1_C2.1_IMPERFECT_SUBJUNCTIVE
- **Title:** The Imperfect Subjunctive (Le Subjonctif Imparfait)
- **Category:** Verbs / Literary Moods
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Understand and recognize the literary imperfect subjunctive used in classical literature, formal discourse, and elevated writing after past main verbs.
- **Prerequisites:** B2.1_SUBJ_PRES, B1.1_IMPARFAIT_FORMATION
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/imperfect-subjunctive/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - Formed from the 3rd person singular *passé simple* stem by appending endings: `-sse`, `-sses`, `-t` (with a circumflex accent over the preceding vowel), `-ssions`, `-ssiez`, `-ssent`.
  - Used in high-register literary French in subordinate clauses governed by a main clause verb in a past tense (*imparfait*, *passé simple*, *plus-que-parfait*, *conditionnel passé*) requiring the subjunctive.
- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *bien que*, *quoique*, *afin que*, *pour que*, *il fallait que*, *avant que*, *de peur que*, *exiger que*.

#### Canonical Sentence Examples
| French Sentence | ES/EN Translation | Targeted Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Il fallait qu'il parlât devant toute l'assemblée. | Era necesario que el hablara / It was necessary that he speak before the entire assembly. | Subjonctif Imparfait (*parlât*) |
| Le roi ordonna qu'ils fussent arrêtés immédiatement. | El rey ordenó que fuesen detenidos / The king ordered that they be arrested immediately. | Subjonctif Imparfait (*fussent*) |
| Quoique nous eussions des doutes, nous avons accepté la proposition. | Aunque tuviésemos dudas / Although we had doubts, we accepted the proposal. | Subjonctif Imparfait (*eussions*) |

#### spaCy Corpus Detection Strategy
- **Linguistic Criteria:** `VERB` or `AUX` token with morphological attributes `Mood=Sub` and `Tense=Imp`, preceded by a subordinate conjunction `que`.
- **spaCy Matcher Pattern:**
```python
patterns = [
    [{"TEXT": {"REGEX": "(?i)(ât|ît|ût|assent|issent|ussent|ions|iez|fût|fussent|fussions|fussiez|eût|eussent|eussions|eussiez)$"}}],
    [{"POS": {"IN": ["VERB", "AUX"]}, "MORPH": {"IS_SUPERSET": ["Mood=Sub", "Tense=Imp"]}}]
]
```
- **Detection Edge Cases & Disambiguation:** 3rd person singular circumflex forms (e.g., *fût*, *pût*, *aimât*, *fît*) must be distinguished from passé simple 3rd person singular by looking for `Mood=Sub` in spaCy morpho-analysis and the preceding `que` subclause conjunction.

### Topic ID: C1_C2.1_PLUPERFECT_SUBJUNCTIVE
- **Title:** The Pluperfect Subjunctive (Le Plus-que-parfait du Subjonctif)
- **Category:** Verbs / Compound Literary Moods
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Master the compound literary subjunctive tense to express completed past actions that occurred prior to another past event under subjunctive-triggering conditions.
- **Prerequisites:** C1_C2.1_IMPERFECT_SUBJUNCTIVE, B2.1_SUBJ_PAST
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/pluperfect-subjunctive/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - Formed using the auxiliary verb (*avoir* or *être*) conjugated in the imperfect subjunctive (*eusse, eusses, eût, eussions, eussiez, eussent* or *fusse, fusses, fût, fussions, fussiez, fussent*) followed by the past participle of the main verb.
  - Used in formal and literary texts for unfulfilled past hypothetical conditions or subordinate past events prior to another past action.
- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *bien que*, *quoique*, *sans que*, *avant que*, *eût*, *fusse*, *si* (in literary inversion without *si*).

#### Canonical Sentence Examples
| French Sentence | ES/EN Translation | Targeted Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Eût-il su la vérité, il n'aurait pas réagi ainsi. | Si hubiera sabido la verdad / Had he known the truth, he would not have reacted like that. | Plus-que-parfait du subjonctif with inversion (*Eût-il su*) |
| Bien qu'elle eût terminé ses études, elle continua d'apprendre. | Aunque hubiese terminado sus estudios / Although she had finished her studies, she continued learning. | Plus-que-parfait du subjonctif (*eût terminé*) |
| Il regrettait qu'ils fussent partis si tôt. | Se lamentaba de que se hubiesen ido tan pronto / He regretted that they had left so early. | Plus-que-parfait du subjonctif (*fussent partis*) |

#### spaCy Corpus Detection Strategy
- **Linguistic Criteria:** `AUX` with `Mood=Sub` and `Tense=Imp` followed (optionally after pronouns/adverbs) by a `VERB` with `VerbForm=Part` and `Tense=Past`.
- **spaCy Matcher Pattern:**
```python
patterns = [
    [
        {"TEXT": {"REGEX": "(?i)^(eût|eussent|eussions|eussiez|fût|fussent|fussions|fussiez)$"}},
        {"OP": "*"},
        {"POS": "VERB"}
    ],
    [
        {"POS": "AUX", "MORPH": {"IS_SUPERSET": ["Mood=Sub"]}},
        {"OP": "*"},
        {"POS": "VERB", "MORPH": {"IS_SUPERSET": ["VerbForm=Part"]}}
    ],
    [
        {"LEMMA": "regretter"},
        {"OP": "*"},
        {"LEMMA": "que"},
        {"OP": "*"},
        {"POS": "VERB"}
    ],
    [{"TEXT": "fussent"}]
]
```
- **Detection Edge Cases & Disambiguation:** Inverted conditional structures (*eût-il*, *fût-elle*) lack an explicit *si* or *que* subclause marker; spaCy matcher must rely on token POS sequence `AUX(Mood=Sub, Tense=Imp) + PRON + VERB(Part)`.

### Topic ID: C1_C2.1_SUBJUNCTIVE_RARE_TRIGGERS
- **Title:** Advanced Subjunctive Triggers & Complex Conjunctions
- **Category:** Syntax / Subjunctive Conjunctions
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Identify and correctly deploy rare, formal, and elevated conjunctions that require the subjunctive mood in academic and literary contexts.
- **Prerequisites:** B2.1_SUBJUNCTIVE_PRESENT, C1_C2.1_IMPERFECT_SUBJUNCTIVE
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/french-word-of-the-day/que/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - High-register conjunctions introduced by or combined with *que* trigger the subjunctive mood: *pour peu que* (if only / provided that), *pourvu que* (provided that), *en sorte que* (so that - with intention), *bien que* / *quoique* (although), *soit que... soit que* (whether... or).
  - Structure: `Conjunction (SCONJ/ADV) + que (SCONJ) + Subject (PRON/NOUN) + Verb (VERB[Mood=Sub])`.
- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *pour peu que*, *pourvu que*, *quoique*, *bien que*, *à condition que*, *jusqu'à ce que*, *en admettant que*.

#### Canonical Sentence Examples
| French Sentence | ES/EN Translation | Targeted Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Pour peu qu'on lui donne une chance, il réussira. | Con tal de que se le dé una oportunidad / Provided one gives him a chance, he will succeed. | Rare trigger *pour peu que* + Subjunctive (*donne*) |
| Pourvu qu'il fasse beau demain, nous ferons de la randonnée. | Ojalá haga buen tiempo mañana / Provided it's nice weather tomorrow, we will hike. | Trigger *pourvu que* + Subjunctive (*fasse*) |
| Quoiqu'il soit fatigué, il poursuit son travail avec ferveur. | Aunque esté cansado / Although he is tired, he continues his work with fervor. | Conjunction *quoique* + Subjunctive (*soit*) |

#### spaCy Corpus Detection Strategy
- **Linguistic Criteria:** Fixed multi-word conjunction expressions (`pour`, `peu`, `que` or `pourvu`, `que`) followed by a verb tagged with `Mood=Sub`.
- **spaCy Matcher Pattern:**
```python
patterns = [
    [{"LEMMA": {"IN": ["peu", "pourvu", "quoique", "condition", "sorte"]}}, {"OP": "*"}, {"LEMMA": {"IN": ["que", "qu'"]}}],
    [{"TEXT": {"REGEX": "(?i)^(quoiqu'|pourvu|pour|quoique)"}}]
]
```
- **Detection Edge Cases & Disambiguation:** *de sorte que* can govern either indicative (expressing an accomplished result) or subjunctive (expressing intended purpose). Disambiguate by checking the mood feature of the subordinate verb.

### Topic ID: C1_C2.1_SUBJUNCTIVE_NE_EXPLÉTIF
- **Title:** Subjunctive with Ne Explétif (Le Ne Explétif)
- **Category:** Syntax / Literary Stylistics
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Apply and recognize the non-negative formal *ne explétif* in formal writing after verbs and conjunctions of fear, avoidance, prevention, and condition.
- **Prerequisites:** C1_C2.1_SUBJUNCTIVE_RARE_TRIGGERS, B1.2_NEGATION_NE_PAS
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/french-word-of-the-day/que/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - The particle *ne* (or *n'*) is inserted before the subjunctive verb without a second negative word (*pas*, *jamais*, *rien*). It carries no negative meaning and serves purely stylistic/formal purpose.
  - Required in formal French after conjunctions: *à moins que*, *de peur que*, *de crainte que*, *avant que*, and after main verbs of fear (*craindre*, *avoir peur*) in affirmative sentences.
- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *de peur que... ne*, *de crainte que... ne*, *à moins que... ne*, *avant que... ne*, *craindre que... ne*.

#### Canonical Sentence Examples
| French Sentence | ES/EN Translation | Targeted Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Je suis parti tôt de peur qu'il ne pleuve. | Me fui temprano por miedo a que lloviera / I left early for fear that it might rain. | *Ne explétif* after *de peur que* (*ne pleuve*) |
| Nous viendrons à moins qu'il ne soit trop tard. | Vendremos a menos que sea demasiado tarde / We will come unless it is too late. | *Ne explétif* after *à moins que* (*ne soit*) |
| Il faut agir avant qu'il ne soit trop tard. | Hay que actuar antes de que sea demasiado tarde / We must act before it is too late. | *Ne explétif* after *avant que* (*ne soit*) |

#### spaCy Corpus Detection Strategy
- **Linguistic Criteria:** Conjunction of fear/condition (*de peur que*, *à moins que*, *avant que*) followed by `ADV` (`ne`/`n'`) and a `VERB` (`Mood=Sub`), WITHOUT any subsequent negative particle (`pas`, `plus`, `jamais`, `rien`).
- **spaCy Matcher Pattern:**
```python
patterns = [
    [
        {"LEMMA": {"IN": ["avant", "peur", "crainte", "moins", "empêcher", "craindre"]}},
        {"OP": "*"},
        {"LEMMA": {"IN": ["que", "qu'"]}},
        {"OP": "*"},
        {"LEMMA": "ne"}
    ]
]
```
- **Detection Edge Cases & Disambiguation:** Verify that no negative adverb (`pas`, `point`, `jamais`, `personne`, `rien`) is attached to the verb to ensure it is true *ne explétif* rather than full negation.

## Module C1_C2.2: Passé Simple & Passé Antérieur

### Topic ID: C1_C2.2_PASSE_SIMPLE_REGULAR_IRREGULAR
- **Title:** The Simple Past Tense (Le Passé Simple)
- **Category:** Verbs / Literary Past Tenses
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Master the formation, recognition, and literary deployment of the *passé simple* tense in historical narratives, literature, and formal texts.
- **Prerequisites:** B1.1_PASSE_COMPOSE, B2.1_IMPARFAIT
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/passe-simple/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - Synthetic literary past tense with no auxiliary verb.
  - Ending groups:
    - `-er` verbs: `-ai`, `-as`, `-a`, `-âmes`, `-âtes`, `-èrent` (e.g., *parla*, *parlèrent*).
    - `-ir` / `-re` verbs: `-is`, `-is`, `-it`, `-îmes`, `-îtes`, `-irent` (e.g., *finit*, *finirent*).
    - Irregular stems: `-u-` pattern (*fut*, *eut*, *voulut*, *sut*, *pût*) and `-ins-` pattern (*vint*, *tint*).
  - Serves the exact aspectual function of *passé composé* (completed past actions) but exclusively in written/literary register.
- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *soudain*, *tout à coup*, *ce jour-là*, *lorsque*, *quand*, *puis*, *aussitôt*.

#### Canonical Sentence Examples
| French Sentence | ES/EN Translation | Targeted Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Le prince marcha vers le château et ouvrit la porte. | El príncipe caminó hacia el castillo y abrió la puerta / The prince walked toward the castle and opened the door. | Passé simple (*marcha*, *ouvrit*) |
| Ils furent surpris par la nouvelle et prirent une décision. | Fueron sorprendidos por la noticia y tomaron una decisión / They were surprised by the news and took a decision. | Passé simple irregular (*furent*, *prirent*) |
| Elle vit la lumière, courut vers elle et s'arrêta. | Vio la luz, corrió hacia ella y se detuvo / She saw the light, ran toward it, and stopped. | Passé simple sequence (*vit*, *courut*, *s'arrêta*) |

#### spaCy Corpus Detection Strategy
- **Linguistic Criteria:** `VERB` or `AUX` token with morphological attribute `Tense=Past` and `VerbForm=Fin` without an accompanying auxiliary verb (`aux:tense`).
- **spaCy Matcher Pattern:**
```python
patterns = [
    [{"POS": {"IN": ["VERB", "AUX"]}, "MORPH": {"IS_SUPERSET": ["Tense=Past", "VerbForm=Fin"]}}],
    [{"POS": {"IN": ["VERB", "AUX"]}, "MORPH": {"IS_SUPERSET": ["Tense=Past"]}}],
    [{"TEXT": {"REGEX": "(?i)(a|èrent|it|irent|ut|urent|int|inrent|fut|furent|eut|eurent|vit|courut|arrêta|marcha|ouvrit)$"}}]
]
```
- **Detection Edge Cases & Disambiguation:** 3rd person singular forms of -ir verbs in *passé simple* (*il finit*) match *présent de l'indicatif* (*il finit*) in spelling. spaCy morpho-parser uses surrounding context (literary tense context, presence of other passé simple forms) to set `Tense=Past`.

### Topic ID: C1_C2.2_PASSE_ANTERIEUR
- **Title:** The Past Anterior Tense (Le Passé Antérieur)
- **Category:** Verbs / Compound Literary Tenses
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Form and correctly utilize the *passé antérieur* to express a completed action that occurred immediately prior to another action expressed in the *passé simple*.
- **Prerequisites:** C1_C2.2_PASSE_SIMPLE_REGULAR_IRREGULAR, B2.1_PLUS_QUE_PARFAIT
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/past-anterior/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - Compound literary tense formed by conjugating the auxiliary verb (*avoir* or *être*) in the *passé simple* (*eus, eus, eut, eûmes, eûtes, eurent* / *fus, fus, fut, fûmes, fûtes, furent*) plus the past participle of the main verb.
  - Used in subordinate time clauses introduced by conjunctions of immediate anteriority (*dès que*, *aussitôt que*, *lorsque*, *quand*, *à peine... que*).
- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *dès que*, *aussitôt que*, *lorsque*, *quand*, *à peine... que*, *une fois que*.

#### Canonical Sentence Examples
| French Sentence | ES/EN Translation | Targeted Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Dès qu'il eut fini son discours, la foule applaudit. | Tan pronto como hubo terminado su discurso / As soon as he had finished his speech, the crowd cheered. | Passé antérieur (*eut fini*) |
| Aussitôt qu'elle fut arrivée, la réunion commença. | Tan pronto como hubo llegado / As soon as she had arrived, the meeting began. | Passé antérieur (*fut arrivée*) |
| A peine eurent-ils franchi le seuil que l'orage éclata. | Apenas hubieron cruzado el umbral / Barely had they crossed the threshold when the storm broke. | Passé antérieur with inversion (*eurent-ils franchi*) |

#### spaCy Corpus Detection Strategy
- **Linguistic Criteria:** Subordinate temporal conjunction (*dès que*, *aussitôt que*) followed by `AUX` (`Tense=Past`, `VerbForm=Fin` in *passé simple*) + `VERB` (`VerbForm=Part`).
- **spaCy Matcher Pattern:**
```python
patterns = [
    [
        {"LEMMA": {"IN": ["dès", "aussitôt", "quand", "lorsque", "peine", "après"]}},
        {"OP": "*"},
        {"POS": "AUX"},
        {"OP": "*"},
        {"POS": "VERB", "MORPH": {"IS_SUPERSET": ["VerbForm=Part"]}}
    ],
    [
        {"TEXT": {"REGEX": "(?i)^(eut|eurent|fut|furent)$"}},
        {"OP": "*"},
        {"POS": "VERB", "MORPH": {"IS_SUPERSET": ["VerbForm=Part"]}}
    ],
    [{"LEMMA": "peine"}, {"OP": "*"}, {"LEMMA": "que"}]
]
```
- **Detection Edge Cases & Disambiguation:** Distinguish *passé antérieur* (*eut fini*) from *plus-que-parfait* (*avait fini*) by checking that the auxiliary morph features specify *passé simple* (`Tense=Past`, `VerbForm=Fin`) rather than *imparfait* (`Tense=Imp`).

### Topic ID: C1_C2.2_HISTORICAL_NARRATIVE_SYNTAX
- **Title:** Advanced Historical & Narrative Syntax (Passé Simple vs Imparfait vs Passé Antérieur)
- **Category:** Discourse / Narrative Structuring
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Master the complex interaction of literary past tenses (*passé simple*, *imparfait*, *passé antérieur*, *plus-que-parfait*) to structure multi-layered historical narratives.
- **Prerequisites:** C1_C2.2_PASSE_SIMPLE_REGULAR_IRREGULAR, C1_C2.2_PASSE_ANTERIEUR
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/passe-simple/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - Narrative foregrounding vs backgrounding in formal/literary prose:
    - *Imparfait*: Descriptive background, ongoing states, habitual actions.
    - *Passé Simple*: Foregrounded sequential events driving the plot forward.
    - *Passé Antérieur*: Immediate anterior event directly preceding a *passé simple* action.
    - *Plus-que-parfait*: Distant background anterior event.
- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *pendant que*, *alors que*, *soudain*, *dès que*, *ce soir-là*, *au moment où*.

#### Canonical Sentence Examples
| French Sentence | ES/EN Translation | Targeted Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Tandis que la nuit tombait sur la ville, le roi rentra dans son palais. | Mientras la noche caía sobre la ciudad, el rey regresó a su palacio. | Imparfait background + Passé simple foreground |
| Quand il eut scellé la lettre, il la remit au messager qui l'attendait. | Cuando hubo sellado la carta, se la entregó al mensajero que lo esperaba. | Passé antérieur + Passé simple + Imparfait relative clause |
| L'armée avançait à couvert car l'ennemi avait déjà occupé la colline. | El ejército avanzaba al abrigo pues el enemigo ya había ocupado la colina. | Imparfait state + Plus-que-parfait background |

#### spaCy Corpus Detection Strategy
- **Linguistic Criteria:** Sentence containing co-occurring literary tenses (`VERB[Tense=Imp]` + `VERB[Tense=Past]` in non-auxiliary dependency).
- **spaCy Matcher Pattern:**
```python
patterns = [
    [{"LEMMA": {"IN": ["tandis", "alors", "car", "lorsque", "pendant"]}}, {"OP": "*"}, {"POS": {"IN": ["VERB", "AUX"]}}],
    [{"POS": {"IN": ["VERB", "AUX"]}, "MORPH": {"IS_SUPERSET": ["Tense=Past"]}}]
]
```
- **Detection Edge Cases & Disambiguation:** Co-occurrence pattern ensures detection of complex narrative compound sentences combining background descriptions with punctual literary actions.

## Module C1_C2.3: Advanced Syntax, Discourse Markers & N'importe/Quelque Expressions

### Topic ID: C1_C2.3_EXPRESSIONS_NIMPORTE
- **Title:** Indefinite Expressions with N'importe (N'importe quel / qui / quoi / où / quand / comment)
- **Category:** Syntax / Indefinite Expressions
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Accurately form and deploy all indefinite expressions with *n'importe* to express complete indifference or universal selection ("any", "whoever", "whatever", "anywhere").
- **Prerequisites:** B1.2_INDEFINITE_PRONOUNS
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/expressions-with-nimporte/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - Formed by *n'importe* + interrogative word/pronoun:
    - *n'importe quoi* (anything / nonsense - pronoun/noun complement).
    - *n'importe qui* (anyone / anybody).
    - *n'importe où* (anywhere).
    - *n'importe quand* (anytime / whenever).
    - *n'importe comment* (anyhow / in any way).
    - *n'importe quel(le)(s)* + NOUN (any + noun - adjective modifying noun).
- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *n'importe quoi*, *n'importe qui*, *n'importe où*, *n'importe quand*, *n'importe comment*, *n'importe quel*.

#### Canonical Sentence Examples
| French Sentence | ES/EN Translation | Targeted Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Tu peux venir n'importe quand, la porte est toujours ouverte. | Puedes venir en cualquier momento / You can come anytime, the door is always open. | Adverbial indefinite *n'importe quand* |
| Il dit n'importe quoi pour se défendre. | Dice cualquier cosa / He says whatever / total nonsense to defend himself. | Pronoun indefinite *n'importe quoi* |
| Choisissez n'importe quel livre sur cette étagère. | Elija cualquier libro / Choose any book on this shelf. | Adjectival indefinite *n'importe quel* + NOUN |

#### spaCy Corpus Detection Strategy
- **Linguistic Criteria:** Compound token sequence starting with particle `n'`, verb `importe` followed by interrogative `quoi`, `qui`, `où`, `quand`, `comment`, or determinant `quel`.
- **spaCy Matcher Pattern:**
```python
pattern = [
    {"LOWER": "n'"},
    {"LOWER": "importe"},
    {"LOWER": {"IN": ["quoi", "qui", "où", "quand", "comment", "quel", "quelle", "quels", "quelles"]}}
]
```
- **Detection Edge Cases & Disambiguation:** *C'est du n'importe quoi* uses *n'importe quoi* as a nominal block meaning "pure nonsense". The pattern correctly catches both adverbial/pronominal and nominal usages.

### Topic ID: C1_C2.3_EXPRESSIONS_QUELQUE
- **Title:** Advanced Indefinite Structures with Quelque (Quelque... que + Subjunctive, Quelconque, Quelque part)
- **Category:** Syntax / Advanced Indefinites
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Master advanced indefinite constructions with *quelque*, including concession clauses (*quelque + adj + que + subj*) and indefinite pronouns.
- **Prerequisites:** C1_C2.1_SUBJUNCTIVE_RARE_TRIGGERS, B1.2_INDEFINITE_PRONOUNS
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/expressions-with-quelque/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - Concessive structure: `quelque` (invariable ADV) + `ADJ` + `que` (SCONJ) + `PRON/NOUN` + `VERB[Mood=Sub]` ("however [adjective]...").
  - Adjectival variable structure: `quelques` + plural noun ("a few").
  - Compound indefinites: *quelqu'un*, *quelque chose*, *quelque part*, *quelques-uns/unes*.
- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *quelque... que*, *quelqu'un*, *quelque chose*, *quelque part*, *quelques-uns*, *un... quelconque*.

#### Canonical Sentence Examples
| French Sentence | ES/EN Translation | Targeted Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Quelque habile que vous soyez, ce problème est complexe. | Por más hábil que sea / However skillful you may be, this problem is complex. | Concessive *quelque + ADJ + que + Subj* (*soyez*) |
| J'ai trouvé quelques-uns de ces documents dans les archives. | Encontré algunos de estos documentos / I found a few of these documents in the archives. | Indefinite pronoun *quelques-uns* |
| Il cherche quelque chose de précis mais se perd quelque part. | Busca algo preciso... en algún lugar / He seeks something precise... lost somewhere. | Compound indefinites *quelque chose*, *quelque part* |

#### spaCy Corpus Detection Strategy
- **Linguistic Criteria:** Token `quelque` followed by `ADJ`, `que`, and a verb in `Mood=Sub` for concessive clauses, or compound tokens `quelqu'un`, `quelque chose`.
- **spaCy Matcher Pattern:**
```python
patterns = [
    [{"LEMMA": "quelque"}, {"OP": "*"}, {"LEMMA": {"IN": ["que", "qu'", "soit", "chose", "part", "un", "uns", "une", "unes"]}}],
    [{"TEXT": {"REGEX": "(?i)^quelque"}}]
]
```
- **Detection Edge Cases & Disambiguation:** Distinguish *quel que* (in two words before *être*: *quels que soient vos motifs*) from *quelque* (in one word before an adjective/noun).

### Topic ID: C1_C2.3_PARTICIPLE_GERUND_STRUCTURES
- **Title:** Participial Clauses, Present Participles & Gerunds (Participe Présent, Gérondif & Participe Passé Absolu)
- **Category:** Syntax / Non-Finite Verb Clauses
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Form and utilize non-finite participial clauses (*participe présent*, *gérondif*, *participe absolu*) to condense subordinate clauses in formal academic French.
- **Prerequisites:** B2.2_GERUNDIVE, B2.1_PARTICIPLE_PRESENT
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/ing-in-french/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - *Participe présent*: Verb stem + `-ant` (invariable verb form, e.g., *sachant*, *ayant*, *étant*, *prenant*). Expresses cause, condition, or simultaneous action without preposition.

  - *Participe absolu*: Independent noun phrase + participle (e.g., *Le repas terminé, ils partirent*).

| La tempête une fois calmée, les marins reprirent la mer. | Calmada la tormenta / The storm once calmed, the sailors set out to sea again. | Participe absolu (*La tempête... calmée*) |

## Module C1_C2.4: Advanced Syntax & Style

### Topic ID: C1_C2.4_INVERSION_STYLISTIQUE
- **Title:** Literary & Formal Subject-Verb Inversion (Inversion Stylistique & Sujet Inversé)
- **Category:** Syntax / Formal Register & Stylistics
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Master literary and formal subject-verb inversion patterns in French, including inversion after initial adverbs, incise/reporting clauses after direct speech, and complex inversion with subject pronoun duplication.
- **Prerequisites:** B1.1_QUESTION_FORMATION, B2.2_ADVANCED_SYNTAX, C1_C2.3_ADVANCED_DISCOURSE_MARKERS
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/asking-questions/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - **Inversion After Initial Adverbs:**
    - When certain restrictive, modal, or connective adverbs open a clause, formal and literary French requires (or strongly favors) subject-verb inversion:
      - *Peut-être*, *sans doute*, *à peine*, *ainsi*, *toujours* (meaning "and so / still"), *en vain*, *du moins*, *au moins*.
    - **Simple Inversion:** If the subject is a pronoun (*il*, *elle*, *on*, *ils*, *elles*, *nous*, *vous*, *je*, *tu*), it is hyphenated directly after the conjugated verb: `Adverb + Conjugated Verb + Pronoun Subject` (*Peut-être viendra-t-il*).
    - Note on euphonic `-t-`: When a 3rd person verb form ends in a vowel (*a*, *e*), euphonic `-t-` is inserted between verb and pronoun (*peut-être décida-t-elle*, *à peine parla-t-il*).
  - **Incise / Reporting Inversion in Quoted Speech:**
    - In dialogue and literary reporting clauses (*proposition incise*), the subject and verb are systematically inverted after or within quoted speech: `« Direct Speech » + Conjugated Verb + Pronoun/Noun Subject` (*« Je viens », dit-il* / *« C'est impossible », s'exclama le professeur*).
  - **Complex Inversion (Subject Pronoun Duplication):**
    - When the subject is a full noun phrase (not a pronoun) and formal inversion is required (either in questions or after initial adverbs like *peut-être*, *sans doute*), the noun remains in initial subject position and is duplicated after the verb by its corresponding 3rd-person subject pronoun (*il*, *elle*, *ils*, *elles*): `Noun Subject + Verb + Pronoun Subject` (*Mon père est-il venu ?* / *Sans doute le ministre tiendra-t-il compte de cette remarque*).

- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *peut-être*, *sans doute*, *à peine*, *ainsi*, *en vain*, *du moins*, *dit-il*, *répondit-elle*, *s'exclama-t-il*.

#### Canonical Sentence Examples
| French Sentence | ES/EN Translation | Targeted Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Peut-être viendra-t-il à la réunion ce soir. | Tal vez venga a la reunión esta noche / Perhaps he will come to the meeting tonight. | Inversion after initial modal adverb *peut-être* with euphonic `-t-` |
| À peine avait-il fini son discours que la foule l'applaudit. | Apenas había terminado su discurso... / Barely had he finished his speech when the crowd applauded him. | Simple inversion after initial temporal adverb *à peine* |
| Ainsi décida-t-elle de quitter la ville sans attendre. | Así decidió ella abandonar la ciudad... / Thus did she decide to leave the city without waiting. | Simple inversion after initial connective adverb *ainsi* |
| Sans doute votre proposition sera-t-elle examinée par le conseil. | Sin duda su propuesta será examinada... / Undoubtedly your proposal will be examined by the council. | Complex inversion (noun duplication *votre proposition... sera-t-elle*) after *sans doute* |
| « Nous devons agir immédiatement », répondit le capitaine. | "Debemos actuar inmediatamente", respondió el capitán / "We must act immediately," replied the captain. | Incise reporting inversion (*répondit le capitaine*) after quoted speech |
| Mon père est-il déjà venu ce matin ? | ¿Ya vino mi padre esta mañana? / Has my father already come this morning? | Formal complex inversion with noun duplication (*Mon père... est-il*) |

#### spaCy Corpus Detection Strategy
- **Linguistic Criteria:**
  - Sequence of an initial adverb (*peut-être*, *à peine*, *ainsi*, *sans doute*, *en vain*, *du moins*) followed by a verb and a hyphenated subject pronoun (`PRON`), OR
  - Sequence of quoted text/punctuation followed by a reporting verb (`VERB`) and its subject noun/pronoun, OR
  - Sequence of a `NOUN` subject followed by a verb and a matching hyphenated subject pronoun (`PRON`).

    [{"TEXT": {"REGEX": "(?i)^(peut-être|sans doute|aussi|à peine|toujours est-il)$"}}],

- **Detection Edge Cases & Disambiguation:**
  - **Hyphenation & Euphonic `-t-` Tokens:** Depending on the spaCy tokenizer configuration for French, hyphenated inverted pronouns like *viendra-t-il* may be split into separate tokens `["viendra", "-t-", "il"]` or `["viendra", "-", "t", "-", "il"]` or kept as a single token. Patterns must account for optional `-t-` tokens.
  - **Question Inversion vs. Initial Adverb Stylistic Inversion:** Both structures share the morphosyntactic verb-pronoun inversion, but initial adverb inversion occurs in declarative sentences preceded by *peut-être*, *à peine*, etc.

### Topic ID: C1_C2.4_COMPLEX_NEGATION
- **Title:** Compound & Literary Negation (Ne...guère, Ne...nul, Ne...nullement, Ne...point & Ne Explétif)
- **Category:** Syntax / Literary Negation & Stylistics
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Synthesize grammatical rules and master formal deployment of compound/literary negative adverbs (*ne...guère*, *ne...nul/nulle*, *ne...nullement*, *ne...point*) and the non-negative *ne explétif* in subordinate clauses following verbs/conjunctions of fear, doubt, prevention, and temporal precedence.
- **Prerequisites:** B1.2_NEGATION_NE_PAS, C1_C2.1_SUBJUNCTIVE_NE_EXPLÉTIF
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/negation/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - **Literary Negative Adverbs & Determiners:**
    - *Ne...guère*: Equivalent to *à peine* ("hardly", "scarcely", "barely"). Replaces *pas* in formal/literary register. Positioned around conjugated verbs (`ne + V + guère`) or before past participles/infinitives.
    - *Ne...point*: Strong literary variant of *ne...pas* ("not at all", "not in the least"). Highly formal or archaic register.
    - *Ne...nullement* / *Ne...aucunement*: Emphatic negative adverbs meaning "not at all", "in no way", "by no means". Function as adverbial modifiers of verbs or adjectives.
    - *Ne...nul / nulle*: Indefinite negative determiner ("no", "not any") or pronoun ("no one", "none"). Agrees in gender with the modified noun.
  - **Ne Explétif in Subordinate Clauses:**
    - The *ne explétif* is a non-negative particle used in elevated style within subjunctive subordinate clauses. It conveys no semantic negation.
    - **Governing Verbs & Expressions:**
      - Verbs of fear/apprehension (*craindre*, *avoir peur*, *redouter*) when used affirmatively: *Je crains qu'il ne vienne* (I fear he will come).
      - Verbs of prevention/avoidance (*empêcher*, *éviter*): *Empêchez qu'il ne parte* (Prevent him from leaving).
      - Verbs of doubt/denial (*douter*, *nier*) when used negatively or interrogatively: *Je ne doute pas qu'il ne réussisse* (I do not doubt that he will succeed).
    - **Governing Conjunctions:**
      - Temporal precedence: *avant que... ne* (before).
      - Fear/Precaution: *de peur que... ne*, *de crainte que... ne* (for fear that / lest).
      - Condition/Exclusion: *à moins que... ne* (unless).

- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *ne...guère*, *ne...point*, *ne...nullement*, *ne...nul*, *craindre que... ne*, *douter que... ne*, *empêcher que... ne*, *avant que... ne*, *de peur que... ne*.

#### Canonical Sentence Examples
| French Sentence | ES/EN Translation | Targeted Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Il n'a guère apprécié cette remarque désobligeante. | Apenas apreció este comentario desobligante / He hardly appreciated this unkind remark. | Literary negation *ne...guère* (hardly/scarcely) |
| Je ne doute point de sa loyauté envers la patrie. | No dudo en absoluto de su lealtad / I do not doubt at all his loyalty to the homeland. | Literary negation *ne...point* (not at all) |
| Cette explication ne me convient nullement. | Esta explicación no me conviene en absoluto / This explanation does not suit me at all. | Emphatic negative adverb *ne...nullement* (in no way) |
| Nul homme n'est censé ignorer la loi. | Ningún hombre se supone que ignora la ley / No man is supposed to ignore the law. | Negative determiner *nul* + *ne* (no / not any) |
| Nous craignons que la situation ne se détériore rapidement. | Tememos que la situación se deteriore rápidamente / We fear that the situation may deteriorate rapidly. | *Ne explétif* after verb of fear (*craindre*) |
| Il a fermé la porte de peur que le vent ne fasse tout envoler. | Cerró la puerta por miedo a que el viento se llevara todo / He closed the door for fear that the wind might blow everything away. | *Ne explétif* after conjunction *de peur que* |
| Le guide est intervenu pour empêcher que l'incident ne prenne de l'ampleur. | El guía intervino para evitar que el incidente creciera / The guide intervened to prevent the incident from escalating. | *Ne explétif* after verb of prevention (*empêcher*) |
| Il faut agir immédiatement avant qu'il ne soit trop tard. | Hay que actuar inmediatamente antes de que sea demasiado tarde / We must act immediately before it is too late. | *Ne explétif* after temporal conjunction *avant que* |

- **Linguistic Criteria:**
  - Detect literary negative particles (`guère`, `point`, `nullement`, `aucunement`, `nul`) preceded by negative adverb `ne`/`n'`.
  - Detect non-negative *ne explétif* in subclauses governed by fear/doubt/prevention verbs (*craindre*, *douter*, *empêcher*) or conjunctions (*avant que*, *de peur que*, *de crainte que*, *à moins que*) followed by `ne`/`n'` and `Mood=Sub` verb WITHOUT secondary negative scope adverbs (`pas`, `plus`, `jamais`, `rien`, `personne`).
- **spaCy Matcher Pattern:**
```python

    [{"LEMMA": "ne"}, {"OP": "*"}, {"LEMMA": {"IN": ["point", "guère", "goutte", "mie", "nul", "nullement", "aucun", "personne", "rien", "jamais"]}}],

- **Detection Edge Cases & Disambiguation:**
  - **Literary Negation vs. Ne Explétif Disambiguation:** Full literary negation (*ne...guère*, *ne...point*, *ne...nullement*) contains a secondary negative particle that negates the proposition. In contrast, *ne explétif* is non-negative and lacks any accompanying negative particle (*pas*, *plus*, *point*, *guère*, *jamais*, *rien*, *personne*).

### Topic ID: C1_C2.5_ELLIPSIS_SUBSTITUTION
- **Title:** Pro-form Substitution & Ellipsis in Discourse (Neuter Pronouns & VP Ellipsis)
- **Category:** Syntax / Discourse & Ellipsis
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Master clause-level pro-form substitution using neuter pronouns (*le*, *en*, *y*) to replace entire propositions or clausal complements, and apply VP ellipsis patterns in formal conversational dynamics.
- **Prerequisites:** B1.2_PRONOUN_EN_Y, B1.2_DIRECT_INDIRECT_OBJECT_PRONOUNS, B2.2_RELATIVE_PRONOUNS_COMPLEX
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/object-pronouns/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - **Neuter *le* as Clausal Pro-form:** The invariable neuter pronoun *le* replaces a complete clause, predicate, infinitive phrase, or attribute introduced by a cognitive, epistemic, or declarative verb (*savoir*, *croire*, *penser*, *espérer*, *dire*, *vouloir*).
    - Example: *Je sais qu'il viendra* $\rightarrow$ *Je le sais*.
  - **Neuter *en* as Clausal/Prepositional Pro-form:** Replaces a clause or infinitive complement introduced by *de* (*douter de*, *être sûr de*, *s'apercevoir de*).
    - Example: *Je ne doute pas qu'il réussisse* $\rightarrow$ *Je n'en doute pas*.
  - **Neuter *y* as Clausal/Prepositional Pro-form:** Replaces a clause or infinitive complement introduced by *à* (*s'attendre à*, *penser à*, *consister à*).
    - Example: *Je m'attendais à ce qu'il vienne* $\rightarrow$ *Je m'y attendais*.
  - **Conversational VP Ellipsis:** In dialogues, the redundant verb phrase is elided and replaced by the neuter pro-form (*Tu viendras ? - Je l'espère* / *Est-ce qu'elle comprend ? - Je le crois*).

- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *savoir*, *espérer*, *croire*, *douter*, *s'attendre*, *neuter le/l'*, *en*, *y*.

#### Canonical Sentence Examples
| French Sentence | ES/EN Translation | Targeted Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Il viendra demain, du moins je le pense. | Él vendrá mañana, al menos eso creo / He will come tomorrow, at least I think so. | Neuter *le* clause substitution with epistemic verb (*je le pense*) |
| Tu penses qu'ils vont réussir ? — Je l'espère. | ¿Crees que van a tener éxito? — Eso espero / Do you think they will succeed? — I hope so. | Conversational VP ellipsis with neuter *l'* (*Je l'espère*) |
| Est-ce qu'elle doute de son succès ? — Non, elle n'en doute pas. | ¿Duda de su éxito? — No, no duda de ello / Does she doubt her success? — No, she doesn't doubt it. | Neuter *en* proposition substitution (*elle n'en doute pas*) |
| Il s'attendait à ce que la situation empire, et je m'y attendais aussi. | Él esperaba que la situación empeorara, y yo también lo esperaba / He expected the situation to worsen, and I expected it too. | Neuter *y* clause substitution (*je m'y attendais*) |

#### spaCy Corpus Detection Strategy
- **Linguistic Criteria:** Neuter pro-form pronoun (`PRON` with `Gender=Masc`, `Number=Sing`, `Person=3` or `LEMMA` $\in$ {*le*, *en*, *y*}) acting as an object complement attached to a cognitive/epistemic verb (*savoir*, *croire*, *penser*, *espérer*, *douter*, *attendre*, *vouloir*).
- **spaCy Matcher Pattern:**

    [{"TEXT": "espère"}],
    [{"TEXT": "l'espère"}]
]
```
- **Detection Edge Cases & Disambiguation:**
  - **Direct Object Pronoun vs. Neuter Pro-form:** Distinguish *Je le vois* (referring to a masculine singular entity *le garçon*) from neuter *Je le sais* (referring to a proposition/fact). Cognitive/epistemic verbs taking *le/l'* without an explicit nominal antecedent in sentence context indicate neuter pro-form substitution.

### Topic ID: C1_C2.5_RESTRICTIVE_CLAUSES
- **Title:** Restrictive & Exclusive Constructions (Le Ne...que, Seulement, Uniquement, Rien que & Exclusions)
- **Category:** Syntax / Restrictive & Exclusive Modifiers
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Master the nuances, stylistic variations, and syntactic constraints of restrictive (*ne...que*, *ne faire que + infinitif*) and exclusive modifiers (*seulement*, *uniquement*, *rien que*, *à l'exception de*, *hormis*, *excepté*) across register levels.
- **Prerequisites:** B1.2_NEGATION_NE_PAS, B2.2_LOGICAL_CONNECTORS
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/negation/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - **The Restrictive *Ne...que* Construction:**
    - Expresses "only" or "nothing but". It is not a negative expression per se, but an exclusive/restrictive focus particle.
    - *Ne* (or *n'*) precedes the verb, and *que* (or *qu'*) immediately precedes the specific constituent being restricted (noun phrase, prepositional phrase, adverb, or infinitive).
    - Scope: When restricting a direct object: `Subject + ne + Verb + que + Direct Object`. When restricting a temporal/locative complement: `Subject + ne + Verb + Preposition + que + Complement` or `Subject + ne + Verb + que + Prepositional Phrase`.
  - **Habitual/Exclusive Action with *Ne faire que + infinitif*:**
    - Expresses "to do nothing but [action]" or "only do [action]".
    - Conjugated *faire* is surrounded by *ne...que*, followed directly by a bare infinitive: `Subject + ne + faire + que + Verb[VerbForm=Inf]`.
  - **Adverbial Restrictives (*Seulement*, *Uniquement*, *Rien que*):**
    - *Seulement* / *Uniquement*: Adverbial alternatives to *ne...que* used without negative particles. *Uniquement* carries higher formal/academic register.
    - *Rien que*: Colloquial/emphatic restrictive modifier meaning "just" or "simply by / nothing short of" (*Rien que d'y penser...*).
  - **Prepositional & Participial Exclusion Markers (*À l'exception de*, *Hormis*, *Excepté*):**
    - *À l'exception de*: Formal prepositional locution introducing an explicit exception ("with the exception of").
    - *Hormis*: Formal/literary preposition governing a noun phrase ("apart from", "except").
    - *Excepté*: Invariable preposition (when preceding a noun) or agreed participle (when postposed) indicating exclusion ("except").

- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *ne...que*, *n'...que*, *ne faire que*, *seulement*, *uniquement*, *rien que*, *à l'exception de*, *hormis*, *excepté*.

#### Canonical Sentence Examples
| French Sentence | ES/EN Translation | Targeted Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Nous n'avons commandé que deux cafés. | Solo hemos pedido dos cafés / We only ordered two coffees. | Restrictive *ne...que* modifying noun phrase complement |
| Elle ne fait que travailler du matin au soir. | No hace más que trabajar... / She does nothing but work from morning to night. | Restrictive verbal construction *ne faire que + infinitif* |
| Ce privilège est uniquement réservé aux membres de l'association. | Este privilegio está únicamente reservado... / This privilege is strictly/only reserved for association members. | High-register restrictive adverb *uniquement* |
| Rien que d'y penser, j'en ai des frissons. | Solo de pensarlo, me da escalofríos / Just thinking about it gives me chills. | Emphatic restrictive idiom *rien que + infinitif/noun* |

### Topic ID: C1_C2.5_ADVANCED_CONCESSION
- **Title:** Advanced Concession Conjunctions (si tant est que, pour autant que, à supposer que, en admettant que, quelque... que)
- **Category:** Syntax / Subjunctive Concession & Hypotheticals
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Master high-register concessive conjunctions requiring the subjunctive mood (*si tant est que*, *pour autant que*, *à supposer que*, *en admettant que*, *quelque... que*) and contrast them with indicative concessives (*bien que* / *quoique* + subjunctive vs. *si tant est que* + high-hypothetical subjunctive vs. indicative concessive markers).
- **Prerequisites:** C1_C2.1_SUBJUNCTIVE_RARE_TRIGGERS, C1_C2.3_EXPRESSIONS_QUELQUE, B2.2_LOGICAL_CONNECTORS
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/subjunctive/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - **Subjunctive Concessive Locutions:**
    - *si tant est que*: Expresses restrictive/doubtful condition or hypothetical concession ("if indeed / provided that / if at all"). Always triggers the subjunctive.
    - *pour autant que*: Expresses restriction or concession based on limited knowledge ("as far as / provided that"). Triggers subjunctive when expressing uncertainty/hypothesis.
    - *à supposer que* / *en admettant que*: Expresses hypothetical concession or assumption ("assuming that / even supposing that"). Governs subjunctive mood.
    - *quelque... que*: Invariable adverbial *quelque* + adjective + *que* + subject + verb in subjunctive ("however [adjective]... may be").
  - **Indicative vs. Subjunctive Concessive Contrast:**
    - Indicative concessives (*même si*, *alors que*, *bien que* in certain non-standard registers or factual opposition locutions like *tout [adjective] que* + indicative) express established facts or realistic contrasts.
    - Subjunctive concessives (*si tant est que*, *à supposer que*, *en admettant que*) introduce a strong element of hypothesis, doubt, or restriction alongside concession.

- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *si tant est que*, *pour autant que*, *à supposer que*, *en admettant que*, *quelque... que*, *subjonctif*.

#### Canonical Sentence Examples
| French Sentence | ES/EN Translation | Targeted Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Il viendra à la réunion, si tant est qu'il soit libéré à temps. | Vendrá a la reunión, si es que queda libre a tiempo / He will come to the meeting, if indeed he is freed in time. | Concessive-hypothetical *si tant est que* + Subjunctive (*soit*) |
| Pour autant que je sache, aucune décision n'a encore été prise. | Que yo sepa / As far as I know, no decision has been made yet. | Restrictive concession *pour autant que* + Subjunctive (*sache*) |
| À supposer qu'elle refuse cette offre, nous devrons élaborer un plan alternative. | Suponiendo que ella rechace esta oferta / Assuming she rejects this offer, we will have to devise an alternative plan. | Hypothetical concession *à supposer que* + Subjunctive (*refuse*) |
| En admettant qu'ils aient raison, cela ne justifie pas leur conduite. | Admitiendo que tengan razón / Even assuming they are right, that does not justify their conduct. | Concessive assumption *en admettant que* + Subjunctive (*aient*) |
| Quelque intelligents qu'ils soient, ils ne peuvent résoudre ce problème sans aide. | Por más inteligentes que sean / However intelligent they may be, they cannot solve this problem without help. | Advanced concessive *quelque + ADJ + que* + Subjunctive (*soient*) |

#### spaCy Corpus Detection Strategy
- **Linguistic Criteria:** Multi-word concessive conjunction locution (`si tant est que`, `pour autant que`, `à supposer que`, `en admettant que`, or `quelque` + `ADJ` + `que`) followed eventually by a `VERB` or `AUX` with morphological feature `Mood=Sub`.
- **spaCy Matcher Pattern:**
```python
pattern = [
    {"LEMMA": {"IN": ["tant", "autant", "supposer", "admettre", "quelque"]}}
]
```
- **Detection Edge Cases & Disambiguation:**
  - **Indicative vs. Subjunctive Concessives:** Ensure the subordinate verb has `Mood=Sub`. Clauses introduced by indicative concessive markers like *même si* govern indicative tenses (`Mood=Ind`), whereas *si tant est que* or *à supposer que* strictly require `Mood=Sub`.
  - **Disambiguating *pour autant que*:** *pour autant* on its own (without *que*) functions as an adverbial phrase meaning "for all that / nonetheles" (*Il n'est pas pour autant satisfait*). Require the presence of *que* + `VERB[Mood=Sub]` to confirm the concessive subclause.

### Topic ID: C1_C2.5_PAST_CONDITIONAL_MIXED
- **Title:** Mixed Conditionals (Les Conditionnels Mixtes: Si + Plus-que-parfait / Imparfait)
- **Category:** Verbs / Complex Hypotheticals & Conditionals
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Synthesize and master mixed conditional structures combining different tense frames across hypothetical clauses to express cross-temporal cause-and-effect relationships (past causes with present consequences, and permanent/present traits with past consequences).
- **Prerequisites:** B1.1_CONDITIONNEL_PRESENT, B1.2_SI_CLAUSES_IMPARFAIT, B2.1_CONDITIONNEL_PASSE, B2.1_PLUS_QUE_PARFAIT
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/conditional-tense/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - **Type 1 Mixed Conditional (Past Cause $\rightarrow$ Present Ongoing Consequence):**
    - **Subordinate *Si* Clause:** `Si + Plus-que-parfait` (`AUX[Imparfait] + VERB[PastParticiple]`). Expresses an unfulfilled condition or action in the past.
    - **Main Clause:** `Conditionnel Présent` (`VERB/AUX[Conditionnel Présent]`). Expresses a present, ongoing result or state resulting directly from that unfulfilled past cause.
    - Structure: `Si + Subject + aux[Imparfait] + verb[Past Participle], Subject + verb[Conditionnel Présent]`.
    - Example: *Si j'avais étudié hier, je connaîtrais la réponse aujourd'hui.*
  - **Type 2 Mixed Conditional (Present/Permanent Trait $\rightarrow$ Past Consequence):**
    - **Subordinate *Si* Clause:** `Si + Imparfait` (`VERB[Imparfait]`). Expresses a general, permanent trait or ongoing present state.
    - **Main Clause:** `Conditionnel Passé` (`AUX[Conditionnel Présent] + VERB[PastParticiple]`). Expresses an action or result in the past that failed to occur due to that present/permanent state.
    - Structure: `Si + Subject + verb[Imparfait], Subject + aux[Conditionnel] + verb[Past Participle]`.
    - Example: *Si elle parlait anglais, elle aurait obtenu le poste la semaine dernière.*
- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *si*, *s'*, temporal anchors (*hier*, *aujourd'hui*, *maintenant*, *autrefois*, *actuellement*, *la semaine dernière*).

#### Canonical Sentence Examples
| French Sentence | ES/EN Translation | Targeted Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Si j'avais étudié hier, je connaîtrais la réponse aujourd'hui. | Si hubiera estudiado ayer, sabría la respuesta hoy / If I had studied yesterday, I would know the answer today. | Mixed Conditional Type 1: *Si* + Plus-que-parfait (*avais étudié*) $\rightarrow$ Conditionnel Présent (*connaîtrais*) |
| Si nous avions pris le train de huit heures, nous serions déjà à Paris. | Si hubiéramos tomado el tren de las ocho, ya estaríamos en París / If we had caught the eight o'clock train, we would already be in Paris. | Mixed Conditional Type 1: *Si* + Plus-que-parfait (*avions pris*) $\rightarrow$ Conditionnel Présent (*serions*) |
| Si elle parlait espagnol courant, elle aurait traduit ce rapport hier. | Si hablara español fluido, habría traducido este informe ayer / If she spoke fluent Spanish, she would have translated this report yesterday. | Mixed Conditional Type 2: *Si* + Imparfait (*parlait*) $\rightarrow$ Conditionnel Passé (*aurait traduit*) |
| Si tu étais plus prudent, tu n'aurais pas eu cet accident le mois dernier. | Si fueras más prudente, no habrías tenido ese accidente el mes pasado / If you were more cautious, you wouldn't have had that accident last month. | Mixed Conditional Type 2: *Si* + Imparfait (*étais*) $\rightarrow$ Conditionnel Passé (*n'aurais pas eu*) |

- **Linguistic Criteria:** Subordinate conjunction `si` / `s'` (`SCONJ`) initiating a clause with a verb in the Plus-que-parfait (auxiliary in Imparfait + past participle) or Imparfait, paired with a main clause verb in the Conditionnel Présent or Conditionnel Passé across clauses. Specifically targeting `si` + PQP verb + Conditionnel Présent main verb for past cause with present outcome.
- **spaCy Matcher Pattern:**
```python
patterns = [
    [{"LEMMA": "si"}, {"OP": "*"}, {"POS": {"IN": ["VERB", "AUX"]}}],
    [{"LEMMA": {"IN": ["vertu", "dépit", "fur", "mesure", "lumière", "faute", "instar", "cadre", "égard"]}}],
    [{"POS": "AUX", "MORPH": {"IS_SUPERSET": ["Mood=Cnd"]}}]
]
```
- **Detection Edge Cases & Disambiguation:**
  - **Standard Past Conditional (PQP $\rightarrow$ Conditionnel Passé):** *Si j'avais étudié, j'aurais réussi* (both clauses in past frame). Distinguish from mixed conditional by confirming main clause verb has `Mood=Cnd`, `Tense=Pres` without an auxiliary forming a compound past conditional.
  - **Standard Present Conditional (Imparfait $\rightarrow$ Conditionnel Présent):** *Si j'étudiais, je réussirais* (both present/future frame). Distinguish by verifying the *si*-clause verb is compound Plus-que-parfait (`AUX[Tense=Imp] + VERB[VerbForm=Part]`).
  - **Clause Order Inversion:** The main clause can precede the *si*-clause (*Je connaîtrais la réponse aujourd'hui si j'avais étudié hier*). For full coverage, matcher logic should allow reversed target order (`VERB[Mood=Cnd, Tense=Pres]` ... `si` + PQP).

## Module C1_C2.6: Lexicogrammar & Collocations
- **Title:** Complex Compound Prepositions (Locutions Prépositives Complexe)
- **Category:** Lexicogrammar / Complex Prepositions
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Synthesize, recognize, and master complex multi-word prepositions (*à l'égard de*, *en dépit de*, *faute de*, *à l'instar de*, *au fur et à mesure de*, *dans le cadre de*, *en vertu de*, *à la lumière de*) used in formal, academic, and administrative discourse.
- **Prerequisites:** B1.2_PREPOSITIONS_BASIC, B2.2_ADVANCED_PREPOSITIONS
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/prepositions/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - Compound prepositions (or locutions prépositives) are fixed multi-word sequences functioning syntactically as single prepositions taking a noun phrase or infinitive complement.
  - Formations and semantic values:
    - **à l'égard de**: "with regard to / towards" (expresses relationship, attitude, or reference to someone/something).
    - **en dépit de**: "in spite of / despite" (expresses concession; synonym of *malgré* but higher register).
    - **faute de**: "for lack of / failing" (expresses cause due to absence/lack; followed by noun or infinitive without article, e.g., *faute de moyens*, *faute de pouvoir*).
    - **à l'instar de**: "following the example of / like / much like" (expresses comparison/model; formal/literary).
    - **au fur et à mesure de**: "as [something] progresses / in proportion to" (expresses gradual progression; followed by noun, or *au fur et à mesure que* + verb).
    - **dans le cadre de**: "in the context of / within the framework of" (expresses administrative or conceptual scope).
    - **en vertu de**: "by virtue of / pursuant to" (expresses legal authority, principle, or rule).
    - **à la lumière de**: "in light of" (expresses justification or analysis based on new facts/evidence).
- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *à l'égard de*, *en dépit de*, *faute de*, *à l'instar de*, *au fur et à mesure de*, *dans le cadre de*, *en vertu de*, *à la lumière de*.

### Topic ID: C1_C2.6_VERB_NOUN_COLLOCATIONS
- **Title:** High-Register Verb-Noun Collocations & Fixed Expressions (Locutions Verbales & Expressions Figées)
- **Category:** Lexicogrammar / Verb-Noun MWEs
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Synthesize, analyze, and deploy high-register multi-word verb-noun collocations and fixed idiomatic expressions (*prendre en compte*, *tenir compte de*, *faire état de*, *porter atteinte à*, *mettre en œuvre*, *rendre compte de*, *prendre du recul*) in formal, academic, legal, and professional contexts.
- **Prerequisites:** C1_C2.6_COMPOUND_PREPOSITIONS, B2.2_ADVANCED_VERB_STRUCTURES
- **Source:** https://raw.githubusercontent.com/amaliatodirascu/PolylexFLE/main/README.md

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - High-register verbal Multi-Word Expressions (MWEs) combine a light/support verb (*prendre*, *tenir*, *faire*, *porter*, *mettre*, *rendre*) with a noun complement and obligatory or optional prepositions/determiners to form a single semantic predicate.
  - Formations and semantic values:
    - **prendre en compte**: "to take into account / to consider" (light verb *prendre* + prep *en* + noun *compte* [+ direct object or *de* phrase]).
    - **tenir compte de**: "to take into account / to factor in" (light verb *tenir* + bare noun *compte* + oblig prep *de/d'/du/des*).
    - **faire état de**: "to report / to state / to cite" (light verb *faire* + bare noun *état* + oblig prep *de/d'/du/des*).
    - **porter atteinte à**: "to undermine / to infringe upon / to harm" (verb *porter* + noun *atteinte* + oblig prep *à/au/aux/à l'*).
    - **mettre en œuvre**: "to implement / to execute / to put into effect" (transitive MWE: *mettre* + prep *en* + noun *œuvre* [+ direct object]).
    - **rendre compte de**: "to account for / to give an account of" (reflexive form *se rendre compte de* = "to realize"; non-reflexive *rendre compte de* = "to report on / account for").
    - **prendre du recul**: "to step back / to gain perspective" (verb *prendre* + partitive determiner *du* + noun *recul*).
  - Syntactic behavior & flexibility:
    - Inflection: The head verb inflects across all tenses, moods, and persons (*nous avons pris en compte*, *il convient de mettre en œuvre*, *elle a rendu compte*).
    - Interventions: Adverbs or negation can intervene between the conjugated verb and the noun/prepositional complement (*mettre rapidement en œuvre*, *ne tenir aucunement compte de*).
    - Passive Voice: Transitive MWEs allow passivization (*Des mesures ont été mises en œuvre*, *Un rapport a été fait*).

- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *prendre en compte*, *tenir compte de*, *faire état de*, *porter atteinte à*, *mettre en œuvre*, *rendre compte de*, *se rendre compte de*, *prendre du recul*.

  - *Adverbial Interventions*: Adverbs may separate the verb from the noun phrase (e.g., *prendre immédiatement en compte*, *mettre efficacement en œuvre*). The pattern inserts `{"POS": "ADV", "OP": "*"}` between the verb lemma and the fixed noun complement.

### Topic ID: C1_C2.6_RHETORICAL_STRUCTURES
- **Title:** Rhetorical & Argumentative Patterns (Correlatives, Concessives & Infinitive Inversions)
- **Category:** Discourse / Rhetorical Structures
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Master high-level rhetorical and argumentative structures (*d'une part... d'autre part*, *non seulement... mais aussi/encore*, *tant... que*, *plus... plus*, *certes... mais*, *avoir beau + infinitif*) to construct nuanced, balanced, and persuasive formal prose.
- **Prerequisites:** C1_C2.3_ADVANCED_DISCOURSE_MARKERS, B2.2_LOGICAL_CONNECTORS
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - **Binary Correlatives & Enumeration (*d'une part... d'autre part* / *non seulement... mais aussi*)**:
    - *d'une part... (et) d'autre part*: Coordinates two clauses or arguments. *d'une part* introduces the first vector of argument, *d'autre part* or *et d'autre part* introduces the parallel second vector.
    - *non seulement... mais aussi / mais encore*: Adds emphasis to a cumulative argument. *non seulement* is followed by a clause or constituent, paired with *mais aussi* or *mais encore* in the subsequent clause.
  - **Quantitative & Proportional Correlatives (*tant... que* / *plus... plus*)**:
    - *tant... que*: Expresses extent, cause-and-effect, or inclusion (*tant [adverb/noun/verb] que...* = both... and / so much... that).
    - *plus... plus...* (or *moins... moins...* / *plus... moins...*): Parallel proportional variation without explicit subordination conjunction (*Plus on travaille, plus on réussit*).
  - **Concessive-Assertive & Inverted Concessive Structures (*certes... mais* / *avoir beau + infinitif*)**:
    - *certes... mais*: Concessive pairing where *certes* concedes an initial point and *mais* reasserts the core counter-argument.
    - *avoir beau + infinitif* (or *beau avoir + infinitif* in non-finite/infinitive clauses): Idiomatic concessive structure meaning "no matter how much X does" or "in vain / despite doing X". Conjugated verb *avoir* (or auxiliary) + *beau* + Infinitive (`VERB[VerbForm=Inf]`).
- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *d'une part*, *d'autre part*, *non seulement*, *mais aussi*, *mais encore*, *tant... que*, *plus... plus*, *certes... mais*, *avoir beau*, *beau avoir*.

#### Canonical Sentence Examples
| French Sentence | ES/EN Translation | Targeted Structure |
|---|---|---|
| D'une part, la réforme stimulera l'économie, et d'autre part, elle créera des emplois. | Por una parte... y por otra parte... / On the one hand, the reform will stimulate the economy, and on the other hand, it will create jobs. | Binary correlative (*d'une part... d'autre part*) |
| Non seulement il a terminé le projet à temps, mais il a aussi dépassé les objectifs. | No solo... sino que también... / Not only did he finish the project on time, but he also exceeded the targets. | Cumulative correlative (*non seulement... mais aussi*) |
| Tant la théorie que la pratique confirment la validité de cette démarche. | Tanto la teoría como la práctica... / Both theory and practice confirm the validity of this approach. | Inclusive correlative (*Tant... que*) |
| Plus le problème est complexe, plus les solutions demandent de la rigueur. | Cuanto más complejo es el problema, más... / The more complex the problem is, the more rigor the solutions require. | Proportional correlative (*Plus... plus*) |
| Certes, cette méthode est coûteuse, mais elle garantit des résultats probants. | Ciertamente... pero... / Admittedly, this method is costly, but it guarantees convincing results. | Concessive-assertive pairing (*Certes... mais*) |
| Il a beau répéter ses explanations, personne ne semble le comprendre. | Por más que repita sus explicaciones... / No matter how much he repeats his explanations, no one seems to understand him. | Idiomatic concession (*avoir beau + infinitif*) |
| Beau avoir étudié le sujet, il manqua de discernement lors du débat. | A pesar de haber estudiado el tema... / Despite having studied the subject, he lacked judgment during the debate. | Non-finite concessive inversion (*beau avoir + infinitif*) |

## Module C1_C2.4: Advanced Syntax & Style

### Topic ID: C1_C2.4_ABSOLUTE_PARTICIPLE
- **Title:** The Absolute Participle Clause (Le Participe Absolu / Proposition Participiale)
- **Category:** Syntax / Stylistics & Detached Clauses
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Form and master absolute participle clauses (*proposition participiale*) to express anterior or simultaneous temporal, causal, or conditional circumstances in formal prose with an independent noun subject and without linking conjunctions.
- **Prerequisites:** C1_C2.3_PARTICIPLE_GERUND_STRUCTURES, B2.1_PARTICIPLE_PRESENT, B1.1_PASSE_COMPOSE
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/ing-in-french/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - **Structure:** `[Subject Noun/Pronoun] + [Participle (Passé / Présent / Composé)]`, set off by commas, detached at the beginning or middle of a sentence.
  - **Subject Independence:** Unlike the gerund (*gérondif*) or simple present participle clause (*participe présent*), the absolute participle clause has its **own distinct subject**, independent of the subject of the main clause (e.g., in *Le soleil couché, les voyageurs s'arrêtèrent*, *Le soleil* is subject of *couché*, while *les voyageurs* is subject of *s'arrêtèrent*).
  - **Absence of Conjunctions:** Expresses time (*une fois...*), cause (*comming/étant donné...*), or condition without subordinating conjunctions (*quand*, *parce que*, *après que*).
  - **Agreement & Tense Relations:**
    - **Past Participle (*Participe Passé Absolu*)**: Expresses anteriority (completed action). Agrees in gender and number with its detached subject noun (*la réunion terminée*, *ses études finies*).
    - **Present / Compound Participle (*Participe Présent / Composé Absolu*)**: Expresses simultaneity (*la nuit tombant*) or completed prior action (*la nuit étant tombée*).
  - **Stylistic Register:** High-register formal, literary, academic, and narrative writing used to streamline and elegantize complex sentence structures.

- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** Detached comma-separated clause with noun + past/present participle, optional modifiers like *une fois*, *à peine*, *le/la/les [NOUN] [PARTICIPLE]*.

#### Canonical Sentence Examples
| French Sentence | ES/EN Translation | Targeted Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Une fois la réunion terminée, chacun regagna son bureau. | Una vez terminada la reunión... / Once the meeting was finished, everyone returned to their office. | Absolute participle clause with past participle (*la réunion terminée*) |
| Le soleil couché, les randonneurs dressèrent leur camp. | Puesta la puesta del sol... / With the sun set, the hikers set up their camp. | Detached absolute participle clause of time (*Le soleil couché*) |
| Ses études finies, elle postula dans une grande entreprise. | Concluidos sus estudios... / Her studies completed, she applied to a major company. | Absolute participle clause expressing anteriority (*Ses études finies*) |
| La nuit tombant, nous avons décidé de rentrer à l'hôtel. | Cayeendo la noche... / Night falling, we decided to return to the hotel. | Absolute participle clause with present participle (*La nuit tombant*) |

#### spaCy Corpus Detection Strategy
- **Linguistic Criteria:** Detached clause containing a `NOUN` or `PROPN` serving as nominal head followed by a `VERB` with `VerbForm=Part` (`Tense=Past` or `Tense=Pres`), delimited by punctuation (`PUNCT` comma or clause boundary). The noun must not be dependent on the main verb.
- **spaCy Matcher Pattern:**
```python

    [{"POS": "NOUN"}, {"POS": "VERB", "MORPH": {"IS_SUPERSET": ["VerbForm=Part"]}}],
    [{"POS": "DET"}, {"POS": "NOUN"}, {"POS": "VERB", "MORPH": {"IS_SUPERSET": ["VerbForm=Part"]}}],
    [{"POS": "DET"}, {"POS": "NOUN"}, {"POS": "ADJ"}],
    [{"POS": "VERB", "MORPH": {"IS_SUPERSET": ["VerbForm=Part"]}}, {"POS": "NOUN"}]
]
```
- **Detection Edge Cases & Disambiguation:**
  - **Participial Clause vs. Relative Clause / Apposition:** Distinguish absolute participial clauses from standard post-nominal adjectival participles (*Le livre publié hier est un succès*) where the noun is the main subject/object. The comma boundary `{"POS": "PUNCT", "LOWER": ","}` and independent subject requirement in the main clause disambiguate absolute clauses.

### Topic ID: C1_C2.4_PRONOMINAL_IDIOMATIC
- **Title:** Pronominal Verbs with Idiomatic Meaning Shifts (Les Verbes Pronominaux Idiomatiques)
- **Category:** Verbs / Idiomatic Clitic Structures
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Master high-register pronominal verbs whose meanings shift dramatically through fixed pronominal clitics (*se*) and adverbial pronouns (*en*, *y*), including *s'en aller*, *en vouloir à*, *s'en prendre à*, *se passer de*, *y tenir*, and *s'y connaître en*.
- **Prerequisites:** B1.2_PRONOUN_EN_Y, B1.2_PRONOMINAL_VERBS
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/reflexive-verbs/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic Rules:**
  - **Idiomatic Shift & Fixed Clitics:** Certain verbs undergo a total or significant semantic shift when combined with reflexive pronouns (*me, te, se, nous, vous*) and adverbial clitics (*en*, *y*). The pronominal and adverbial clitics are non-extractable components of the idiomatic verb unit.
  - **Key Expressions & Structures:**
    - **s'en aller** (*to leave / to go away*): Pronominal clitic (*se*) + adverbial pronoun (*en*) + *aller*. Replaces *partir*. In compound tenses: *je m'en suis allé(e)*.
    - **en vouloir à (quelqu'un)** (*to hold a grudge against / to be mad at someone*): Fixed clitic *en* + *vouloir* + preposition *à* + indirect object.
    - **s'en prendre à (quelqu'un/quelque chose)** (*to blame / to attack / to lash out at*): Reflexive clitic (*se*) + fixed clitic *en* + *prendre* + preposition *à*.
    - **se passer de (quelqu'un/quelque chose)** (*to do without / to dispense with*): Reflexive clitic (*se*) + *passer* + preposition *de*. (Contrast with non-pronominal *passer de* or simple *se passer* = to happen).
    - **y tenir** (*to care deeply about / to be attached to*): Fixed adverbial pronoun *y* + *tenir* (+ *à* + noun/infinitive). Expresses strong attachment or insistence.
    - **s'y connaître en (quelque chose)** (*to be an expert in / to know a lot about*): Reflexive clitic (*se*) + fixed clitic *y* + *connaître* + preposition *en*.
  - **Word Order & Clitic Placement:**
    - Present/Imperfect: `Subject + (ne) + reflexive clitic + en/y + Verb + (pas)` (e.g., *Je ne m'en vais pas*, *Il s'y connaît*).
    - Passé Composé: `Subject + reflexive clitic + en/y + être/avoir + Past Participle` (e.g., *Elle s'en est prise à lui*, *Ils s'en sont allés*). Note that past participle agreement follows standard pronominal rules (e.g., *s'en aller* uses *être* and agrees with subject).
    - Imperative Affirmative: `Verb-reflexive pronoun-en/y` (e.g., *Va-t'en !*, *Allons-nous-en !*).

- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *s'en aller*, *en vouloir à*, *s'en prendre à*, *se passer de*, *y tenir*, *s'y connaître en*, *va-t'en*, *m'en vais*, *s'en est pris*.

| Il est tard, je dois m'en aller maintenant. | Es tarde, debo irme ahora / It is late, I must leave now. | Idiomatic verb *s'en aller* (*m'en aller*) |
| Ne lui en veux pas, il ne savait pas ce qu'il faisait. | No le guardes rencor... / Don't hold a grudge against him, he didn't know what he was doing. | Idiomatic structure *en vouloir à* |
| Pourquoi t'en prends-tu toujours à ton frère ? | ¿Por qué te la agarras siempre con tu hermano? / Why do you always blame / lash out at your brother? | Idiomatic verb *s'en prendre à* |
| Nous ne pouvons pas nous passer de cet outil informatique. | No podemos prescindir de esta herramienta... / We cannot do without this IT tool. | Idiomatic verb *se passer de* |
| J'y tiens énormément, c'est un souvenir de ma grand-mère. | Me importa muchísimo / I care about it deeply, it's a keepsake from my grandmother. | Fixed clitic structure *y tenir* |
| Elle s'y connaît en informatique et saura résoudre le problème. | Ella es experta en informática... / She is an expert in IT and will know how to solve the problem. | Idiomatic structure *s'y connaître en* |

### Topic ID: C1_C2.4_REGISTER_VARIATION
- **Title:** Register & Stylistic Variation (Soutenu Syntax vs Informal Spoken Shortcuts)
- **Category:** Stylistics & Register / Syntax Variations
- **Pedagogical Objective:** Master the syntactic, morphological, and phonological contrasts between elevated formal French (*style soutenu*) and informal spoken shortcuts (*registre familier/familier parlé*), identifying formal introductory locutions and spoken elisions.
- **Prerequisites:** B1.2_NEGATION_NE_PAS, B2.1_INDIRECT_DISCOURSE, C1_C2.3_ADVANCED_DISCOURSE_MARKERS
- **Source:** https://www.frenchlearner.com/grammar/

#### Grammatical Breakdown
- **Morphosyntactic & Stylistic Rules:**
  - **Formal Syntax (*Style Soutenu / Académique*)**:
    - **Impersonal Introductory Locutions**: Elevated writing utilizes formal impersonal turns of phrase:
      - *Il convient de + Infinitif* (It is appropriate/fitting to...).
      - *Il est à noter que + Indicatif* (It is to be noted that...).
      - *Il appert que + Indicatif* (It appears / is evident that... - archaizing/legal register).
    - **Formal Topicalization Markers**: *Quant à + Noun/Pronoun* and *En ce qui concerne + Noun* structure formal topic shifts at the head of sentences.
    - **Strict Syntactic Preservations**: Complete preservation of the negative particle *ne*, full pronoun forms (*il y a*, *tu as*, *cela*), and complex subject-verb inversion (*Est-il concevable...*).
  - **Informal Spoken Shortcuts (*Registre Familier / Spoken Elisions*)**:
    - **Dropped *Ne* (*Omis de la négation*)**: Systematic deletion of the negative particle *ne* in spoken French, leaving only *pas*, *plus*, *jamais*, *rien*, etc. (*J'sais pas*, *C'est pas vrai*).
    - **Contraction *Y'a* for *Il y a***: Truncation of the impersonal existential pronoun *il* into a single phonetic unit *y'a* (*Y'a un problème*, *Y'a pas de quoi*).
    - **Phonetic Truncations & Pronoun Elisions**:
      - *Tu* + vowel $\rightarrow$ *T'* (*T'as vu ?*, *T'es sûr ?*).
      - *Je* + consonant/sibilant $\rightarrow$ *J'* (*J'pense*, *J'sais*).
      - *Il* / *Ils* $\rightarrow$ *I'*, *Elle* / *Elles* $\rightarrow$ *E'* (*I' mangent*, *E' sont là*).

- **Key Trigger Words / Markers:** *il convient de*, *il est à noter que*, *quant à*, *en ce qui concerne*, *il appert que*, *y'a*, *y'a pas*, *t'as*, *t'es*, *j'sais pas*.

| Il est à noter que les résultats dépassent nos prévisions initiales. | Cabe señalar que los resultados superan nuestras previsiones / It is to be noted that results exceed initial forecasts. | Formal introductory marker (*Il est à noter que*) |
